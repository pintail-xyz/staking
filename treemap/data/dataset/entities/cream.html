How much ETH CREAM Finance has had in each role recorded in this dataset, week by week, from the start of the beacon chain (1 December 2020) to 20 November 2026. Stake is the effective balance of active validators.

  • Allocator: chose where the stake went. Counts the stake in the treemap boxes under CREAM Finance. Recorded for 89% of active stake.
  • Operator: ran the validators. Recorded for 80% of active stake.
  • Custodian: held the stake on behalf of a client. Recorded for 5.3% of active stake.
  • Principal: owned the staked ETH. Recorded for 6.2% of active stake.

Allocators are recorded for most stake. Operators, custodians and principals are recorded only where specific evidence names them, so their figures here (dashed on the charts) are lower bounds: no figure does not mean no role.
