node operator stakin

Owned by The Tie since 2026-01-12 (decision). From that date its stake also counts towards The Tie's, shown on The Tie's page with its subsidiaries; the figures below are its own.

How much ETH Stakin has had in each role recorded in this dataset, week by week, from the start of the beacon chain (1 December 2020) to 13 November 2026. Stake is the effective balance of active validators.

  • Allocator: chose where the stake went. Counts the stake in the treemap boxes under Stakin. Recorded for 89% of active stake.
  • Operator: ran the validators. Recorded for 51% of active stake.
  • Custodian: held the stake on behalf of a client. Recorded for 5.3% of active stake.
  • Principal: owned the staked ETH. Recorded for 6.2% of active stake.

Allocators are recorded for most stake. Operators, custodians and principals are recorded only where specific evidence names them, so their figures here (dashed on the charts) are lower bounds: no figure does not mean no role.
