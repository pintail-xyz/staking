Lido's operator group 4.8 ("Twinstake") and the operators it lists are run by Twinstake, from the operator's transfer from Nethermind.

Operator or ownerOperated by Twinstake from 2025-10-02, for Twinstake, Twinstake, Twinstake - PGO · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's operator group 4.8 ("Twinstake") and the operators it lists are run by Twinstake from 2 October 2025.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.8, "Twinstake", listing operators 1/25 and 4/8
  • First partyNethermind and Twinstake proposed moving operator 1/25's validators from Nethermind to Twinstake in July 2025
  • On chainThe registry renamed operator 1/25 from "Nethermind" to "Twinstake" on 2 October 2025

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.8, "Twinstake", from 31 July 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/25 ("Twinstake"), 4/8 ("Twinstake - PGO"). Operator 1/25 was registered as "Nethermind" on 24 June 2022. Nethermind and Twinstake, a staking provider Nethermind founded as a joint venture, proposed in July 2025 moving its validators to Twinstake's infrastructure; after Lido's vote the registry renamed it "Twinstake" on 2 October 2025. The group's operators are recorded as run by Twinstake from that date, and operator 1/25 as Nethermind's before it (decision 0317).

What would change it

A Lido, Nethermind or Twinstake statement that these operators belong to another company, or that the transfer took effect on another date.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
