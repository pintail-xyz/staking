Explanation

The contract 0x4bef…9e9d is the staking contract of uniETH, the liquid staking token of Bedrock. Validators whose first deposit was sent to it, and those withdrawing to the addresses those deposits name, are attributed to Bedrock uniETH as allocator. 2,380 validators were deposited from October 2022, holding as much as 49k ETH in June 2024; 316 were active in October 2026, all previously unattributed.

Identification. Bedrock's repository lists this contract as uniETH's mainnet staking contract, together with its restaking contract and an EigenLayer pod. Bedrock's documentation gives the same contract as the place to read the total ETH staked for uniETH. Its code stakes pending ETH whenever users' minting brings the balance to 32 ETH.

On chain. The validators first withdrew to the staking contract itself (369 of them, from October 2022 to January 2024), then to the EigenLayer pod named in the repository, owned by the restaking contract (1,506 from January 2024), and then to nine more pods owned by proxies the restaking contract created. Most blocks carry plain client version graffiti, and the fee recipient is the staking contract itself.

Bedrock chooses the operators of its pool, so it is the allocator; no operator is recorded. RockX is also one of Lido's node operators, a separate role not merged here. The decision would change with a Bedrock statement naming another contract, or evidence that deposits sent to it are not uniETH's.

What would change it

A Bedrock statement naming another contract, or evidence that deposits sent to it are not uniETH's.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
