Explanation

The account 0xb2c4…2fdb made the deposits for the crETH2 staking of CREAM Finance, a lending protocol: validators withdrawing to the address they moved to are attributed to CREAM as allocator. 786 validators withdrew there; they made up 2.1% of all stake at the beacon chain's launch, and all exited in April and May 2023.

What CREAM published. CREAM's documentation lists every deposit transaction of its migration to Ethereum 2.0, seventeen transactions covering validators 0 to 786, and names its crETH2 contract. CREAM ran the validators for crETH2 holders as a custodial service, for 8% of the rewards.

On chain. The seventeen listed transactions deposited 787 validators between November 2020 and June 2021, all with BLS credentials; this account sent thirteen of them. 786 of the validators were later changed to 0x1cd9…62ed, and they are every validator that address has ever had.

CREAM chose and ran the validators for its token holders, so it is the allocator. No operator is recorded. Explorer labels and community label sets are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that the account deposited for others, or a CREAM statement contradicting it.
