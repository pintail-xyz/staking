Explanation

The contract 0xf007…5227 is Figment's batch deposit contract for validators with 0x01 credentials: validators first deposited through it are attributed to Figment as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. 44,665 validators were deposited through it between August 2023 and October 2026, from 2,292 sending accounts to 2,309 withdrawal addresses; about 26,100 were active in October 2026 with some 0.85M ETH.

Identification. Figment's documentation names this contract as its batch deposit contract for 0x01 validators, deployed in July 2023. Its verified source is Figment's FigmentEth2Depositor contract.

Why Figment is the allocator. A client depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider, who generates the keys and runs the validators; the client keeps the withdrawal address. This is the rule applied to all providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005). Among the clients was Celsius, whose validators deposited here are therefore Figment's, with Celsius holding the withdrawal address (decision 0028).

Limits. The contract's deposit function is open to anyone while it is not paused, so a deposit through it does not by itself prove that Figment runs the validator. The decision would change with a Figment statement contradicting it, or evidence that some depositors use the contract with keys Figment does not run.

What would change it

A Figment statement contradicting it, or evidence some depositors use it with keys Figment does not run.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
