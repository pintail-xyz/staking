Dataset › Decisions › 0340-upbit-runs-its-staking

Upbit runs the validators of its ETH staking itself, as its operator Dunamu reports, and they run as one fleet.

Operator or ownerService run by Upbit from 2022-01-03, for Upbit staking · decided 2026-11-19

Validators that Upbit's staking allocates are run by Upbit, from its first ETH staking deposit (3 January 2022).

Grounds

  • First partyDunamu reports that Upbit Staking runs its own validators and nodes, starting with ETH staking in 2022
  • First partyDunamu says Upbit stakes through validators it runs itself and entrusts its members' assets to no outside company
  • On chain98.7% of Upbit's blocks over 51 weeks ran the client build the rest of the fleet ran that week
  • On chainNo block of Upbit's validators names another operator in its graffiti

Explanation

Upbit runs the validators of its ETH staking. Every validator the treemap places in Upbit's staking (decision 0069: those withdrawing to 0x7e2a…2d05, about 20,350 active validators with 0.65M ETH in November 2026) is recorded as run by Upbit, unless other evidence names a different operator.

What Upbit says. Dunamu, which operates Upbit, states in its half-year report for 2026, filed with Korea's Financial Supervisory Service, that Upbit Staking runs its own validators and blockchain nodes, and that it began with Ethereum staking in 2022; its 2025 annual report says the same. In a release of 24 June 2026 Dunamu adds that Upbit Staking is entirely run by Upbit: it does not entrust its members' delegated assets to outside companies, but stakes them through validators it operates itself. The release is about Polygon, but makes the statement of Upbit Staking as a whole, and also gives Upbit's rank in Ethereum staking.

What the chain shows. The validators run as one fleet. In the blocks recorded here (from August 2024), their graffiti is empty until December 2025 and from then on carries only the execution and consensus client versions, which the whole fleet changes together: Reth with Lighthouse briefly, then Geth with Lighthouse through four successive builds. Over the 51 weeks with such graffiti, 98.7% of Upbit's blocks ran the build that most of the fleet ran that week; the weeks below that are changeovers from one build to the next. No block names another operator. Their blocks pay their fees either to Upbit's own withdrawal address or directly to block builders.

From when. From Upbit's first ETH staking deposit, on 3 January 2022: Dunamu says Upbit has run its own validators since it began staking ETH that year.

What would change it

Upbit or Dunamu naming another company as running these validators, or a part of this stake running a fleet distinct from the rest.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
