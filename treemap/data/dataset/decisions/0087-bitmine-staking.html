Explanation

Validators withdrawing to these 111 addresses are BitMine Immersion Technologies' own treasury staking. In October 2026 they numbered about 1,800, with some 3.1M ETH, most of them compounding (0x02) validators of around 1,900 ETH each, deposited to the address that sent them.

Common control. On 31 August 2026, between 13:26 and 14:39 UTC, 209 accounts each sent 0.001 ETH to one of two newly created accounts that have never sent a transaction. Sending from an account requires its key (for a BitGo custody wallet, its signers), so the senders are controlled by one holder or by parties acting together. All 111 addresses are among them: 93 sent directly, and 18 wallets held at BitGo sent through the same account that made their deposits. The other senders include 74 of the 81 addresses of decision 0115 and 24 further BitMine wallets.

Identification with BitMine. No document names these addresses; the identification rests on BitMine's published holdings. Its weekly release of 31 August 2026 gives 5,901,112 ETH held and 5,067,309 ETH staked as of 30 August. On 31 August the 209 senders held 732,732 ETH in their balances and 4,965,499 ETH in active validators; with the seven addresses of decision 0115 that did not take part, the staked total is 5,034,487 ETH, 0.6% below BitMine's figure, and the total held is 3% below it. Earlier releases (4 January 2026, 22 March 2026) match the deposits of these addresses and those of 0115 week by week.

Who runs the validators. The validators fall into several fleets, told apart by the client versions recorded in their blocks. About 0.94M ETH of this stake is on a fleet running a Nethermind build seen on no other validators, the fleet described under decision 0115. About 1.42M ETH runs as SSV clusters on twelve private operators owned by one account. That account was funded from the same source as the SSV operators of Staked's own validators, so these validators are recorded as run by Staked (since 30 October 2026; tag ssv-operator-owner-staked). Since September 2026 some of these validators have also proposed outside SSV, with Lighthouse's default graffiti. About 0.47M ETH runs Nimbus with its default graffiti, and about 0.16M ETH runs the same Nethermind and Lighthouse releases as Lido's operator MAVAN, though those releases are widely used. BitMine owns MAVAN, the staking platform it launched in March 2026; its subsidiary MAVAN Holdings bought the staking provider Pier Two that month (quarterly report). None of the other fleets is shown to be MAVAN's, and BitMine said in January 2026 that it was working with three staking providers. Until a provider is identified for a fleet, BitMine is recorded as the allocator of all of them.

Limits. The match with BitMine's figures is close but not exact, compares effective balances with reported totals, and leaves out pending deposits. A custodian running one control check across several clients' wallets would also produce the 31 August transfers, though 16 of the senders are unstaked wallets whose only transaction is that transfer. Explorer labels naming the addresses as BitMine's are recorded as leads. The decision would change if a provider is identified for these validators, or if any address is shown to be held by someone other than BitMine.

What would change it

A provider identified for these validators, or evidence that an address is held by someone other than BitMine.
