Celsius's two staking accounts: Celsius chose the providers (Blockdaemon, Figment) or staked directly, and received the withdrawals.

AttributionPrincipal Celsius · decided 2026-10-23

Celsius was the principal of the validators withdrawing to these two addresses: it hired their allocators, or staked itself.

Grounds

  • On chainEach address sent its own deposits and withdraws to itself, so whoever holds it chose the staking route and got the ETH back
  • On chainBoth were first funded by the same wallet, and after the exits sent their ETH to the same two wallets
  • Third partyDecision 0162 ties those wallets to the CEL token cited in the court examiner's report on Celsius

Explanation

Celsius, the crypto lender, was the principal of the validators withdrawing to its two staking accounts, 0xeee2…e4c8 and 0x1ced…9074: the ETH was Celsius's, and Celsius chose how it was staked. Where it staked through a provider, the provider is the allocator: Blockdaemon for the validators 0xeee2…e4c8 deposited through Blockdaemon's contract (decision 0041), and Figment for all the validators of 0x1ced…9074, deposited through Figment's contracts (decisions 0029 and 0031). The validators 0xeee2…e4c8 deposited directly are Celsius's own (decision 0162). This decision records Celsius as principal of all 25,344 validators, so Celsius's stake can be shown without counting it twice. All exited by January 2024.

Common control. Each account sent its own deposits and is its own withdrawal address, so whoever holds it chose the staking route and received the ETH back. 0x1ced…9074 was first funded by the same trading wallet that first funded 0xeee2…e4c8, and received most of its ETH from that wallet and others in the same network; the two accounts also sent each other ETH. After the exits, both sent their ETH to the same two wallets, each making its last large transfer on 23 January 2024.

The link to Celsius. Decision 0162 traces those wallets to the owner of Celsius's CEL token, which the examiner's report in Celsius's bankruptcy cites, and which only the issuer would control. Explorer labels on the wallets are recorded as leads, not relied on.

The decision would change with evidence that either account held stake for someone other than Celsius, or that the wallets handling CEL are not Celsius's.

What would change it

Evidence that either address held stake for someone other than Celsius, or that the CEL-handling wallets are not Celsius's.
