Explanation

The contract 0xa863…9cba6 is XHash's batch deposit contract: validators first deposited through it are attributed to XHash as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. It deposited 3,923 validators between March 2023 and August 2025, for 32 sending accounts, holding as much as 65k ETH in May 2024; 117 were active in October 2026, all previously unattributed.

Identification. XHash's documentation for its non-custodial staking, in which XHash generates the keys and the staker connects a wallet and stakes multiples of 32 ETH, names this address as the XHash batch deposit contract. The script xhash.com serves for staking builds each deposit transaction to the same address. The contract's creator remains its owner.

The operators. Most of the validators' blocks carry SSV's default graffiti, with "XHash.com" and other operators' names on the rest: XHash runs most of these validators as SSV clusters with itself among the operators, which is its own choice of operators, so it remains the allocator.

Why XHash is the allocator. A client depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider, who generates the keys and runs the validators; the client keeps the withdrawal address (decision 0005). XHash's "Quick Mode" deposits go directly to the beacon deposit contract and are not covered here; they are identified by XHash's fee recipient (decision 0228). The contract is open to anyone, though in practice it is used only through XHash's application. The decision would change with an XHash statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys XHash does not run are deposited through it.

What would change it

An XHash statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys XHash does not run are deposited through it.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
