Explanation

The contract 0xa2e3…2e1 is Binance's wBETH (Wrapped Binance Beacon ETH) on Ethereum: a token for ETH staked with Binance, valued at an exchange rate Binance publishes. The decision is used by the "Forms of ETH" page, which counts wBETH's supply at its exchange rate as Binance's issue; it also links Binance's staking to Binance (decision 0006).

Identification. BNB Chain's announcement of wBETH lists this as wBETH's Ethereum contract. Binance's own pages could not be retrieved.

On chain. The contract names a receiving account, 0x26ad…6b86, for the ETH paid in to mint wBETH. All 67 of that account's transactions sent ETH to a single address, 1,857,295 ETH in all, and nowhere else. The account holding the contract's operator role was first funded by that same address. In October 2026 the exchange rate was about 1.11 ETH per wBETH, and the supply on Ethereum was 3.18M wBETH.

ETH waiting in the receiving account is counted under wBETH rather than as plain ETH, since it is already in wBETH's supply. An explorer label naming the destination address as Binance's is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with Binance naming another contract as its Ethereum wBETH, or the ETH paid in going somewhere other than Binance.

What would change it

Binance naming another contract as its Ethereum wBETH, or the ETH paid in going somewhere other than Binance.
