Lido's operator group 4.29 ("Solstice") and the operators it lists are run by Solstice, from its acquisition of Bridgetower's staking division.

Lido's operator group 4.29 ("Solstice") and the operators it lists are run by Solstice from 16 December 2024.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.29, "Solstice", listing operators 1/17 and 4/35
  • First partySolstice announced on 16 December 2024 that it had acquired Bridgetower Capital's staking division
  • On chainOperator 1/17 was registered as "BridgeTower" in 2022 and renamed "Solstice" on 31 January 2025

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.29, "Solstice", from 6 August 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/17 ("Solstice"), 4/35 ("Solstice - PTO"). Operator 1/17 was registered as "BridgeTower" on 14 January 2022; Solstice Staking announced on 16 December 2024 that it had acquired Bridgetower Capital's staking division, including its team, and the registry renamed the operator "Solstice" on 31 January 2025. The group's operators are recorded as run by Solstice from 16 December 2024, and operator 1/17 as Bridgetower's before then (decision 0316).

What would change it

A Lido or Solstice statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
