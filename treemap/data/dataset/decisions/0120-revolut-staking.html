Grounds

  • Third partyThe issuer of the Revolut Euro (EURR) stablecoin names Revolut its sole distributor
  • On chainThe wallet that supplied all the depositing account's ETH held EURR before launch and received EURR from the issuance wallet
  • On chainThat account made every deposit, and the validators' withdrawals all return to it

Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x2b78…7470 are Revolut's staking, with Revolut as allocator; the stake is its customers'. 8,834 validators were deposited between September 2022 and September 2026; about 3,585 were active in October 2026 with some 0.11M ETH.

The deposits. One account, 0xc44b…11c3, made every deposit to the address, and no one else has deposited to it. The account is used only for staking (its other transactions are delegations and bridging on other networks), and its ETH comes from only two places: 0x9b0c…8d46, a high-volume exchange hot wallet, and the withdrawal address itself, which sends it every withdrawal to be restaked or returned. The withdrawal address is its validators' own fee recipient, and their graffiti shows client versions only.

The link to Revolut through EURR. Revolut publishes no Ethereum addresses, and its annual reports give no staking figures. The link runs through EURR, the euro stablecoin Bridge issues for Revolut: Bridge's transparency page lists the Revolut Euro token, and its white paper under MiCA names Revolut's European digital-asset company as the sole distributor, offering it through the Revolut app and Revolut X. The token launched publicly in late August 2026. The hot wallet 0x9b0c…8d46 received EURR test tokens in March 2026, months before launch, when only the issuer and its distributor would hold them; from August 2026 it received EURR from the wallet that received almost all of the supply minted, and it passes EURR to a wallet that pays many end users.

So the stake is funded by, and returns to, a wallet that handled a stablecoin only Revolut distributes, from before its launch. A wallet of the exchange depositing for itself makes Revolut the allocator.

Limits. No Revolut document names a wallet. The wallet that received most of the EURR minted may be Bridge's or Revolut's; the argument holds either way. Explorer labels and community label sets naming the accounts as Revolut's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Revolut statement contradicting it, or evidence that the validators are run for a client or chosen by a staking provider.

What would change it

A Revolut statement contradicting it, or evidence that the validators are run for a client or chosen by a staking provider.
