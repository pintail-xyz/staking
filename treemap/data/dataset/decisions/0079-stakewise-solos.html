Explanation

The contract 0xeadc…6ceca ran StakeWise's Solos service, in which StakeWise ran standalone validators for users who kept their own withdrawal key. Validators first deposited through it are attributed to StakeWise as allocator. 381 validators were deposited through it, making up 0.14% of all stake at their peak; all have since exited.

Identification. The contract's verified source is Solos, whose source in StakeWise's repository describes it: users can create standalone validators with their own withdrawal key through this contract, for a monthly price, with StakeWise's operators registering the validators. On chain, it was created by the account that created 15 of the 60 contracts StakeWise lists as its mainnet contracts.

Why StakeWise is the allocator. Users chose StakeWise's service, and StakeWise's operators registered and ran the validators, while the users kept the withdrawal key; this is the rule applied to providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005). No operator is recorded. An explorer label is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with evidence that the contract was used with keys StakeWise did not run.

What would change it

Evidence the contract was used with keys StakeWise did not run.
