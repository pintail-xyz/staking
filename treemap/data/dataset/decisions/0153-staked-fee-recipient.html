Explanation

The address 0xe94f…6ca0c is Staked's execution-reward pool: Staked's validators pay their transaction fees and MEV to it, and it pays them on to stakers after Staked's commission. Validators otherwise unattributed that paid it are attributed to Staked as allocator for as long as they did. Staked has been part of Kraken since 2021. 3,946 validators have paid it since the Merge; in October 2026 about 2,800 relied on this decision, with some 0.11M ETH.

Identification. Staked's FAQ on withdrawals explains that it collects its validators' execution-layer rewards and pays them on, keeping its commission. The validators deposited by the accounts that used Staked's own batch deposit contract (its repository; decisions 0047 to 0066) pay this address. Its first incoming transfer came from a wallet funded by the creator of Staked's batch contract, which has since received 800 ETH from it in commission, and it has made 9,066 payments on to stakers.

The other validators. 2,858 validators not otherwise attributed paid it in 98.6% of their blocks, as the fee recipient their operator set. Most are registered on SSV under one owner account, which also owns validators deposited through Staked's accounts. This matches Staked's move of its fleet to SSV in early 2025, visible in its validators' graffiti. 19 validators otherwise attributed to Bitfinex paid it in 41% of their blocks; they keep their Bitfinex attribution, since a fee recipient yields to every other allocator claim.

Staked is the allocator for its clients, who keep their own withdrawal addresses. The decision would change with evidence that clients of other providers also use this address as their fee recipient.

What would change it

Evidence that clients of other providers also use this address as their fee recipient.
