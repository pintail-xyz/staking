Explanation

Validators that pay their block fees to 0xcda9…7062 and withdraw to these addresses are Gate's ETH staking, with Gate, the exchange, as allocator. In October 2026 there were about 5,700 active validators with some 0.18M ETH. Gate offers ETH staking to its customers, whose stake this is.

The fleet. Since 2025 every block these validators proposed through a relay names the same fee recipient, 0xcda9…7062, and their graffiti is blank or a client version string. That fee recipient pays ETH to each of the five deposit accounts and to the withdrawal addresses. Each withdrawal address sends its ETH only back to its own deposit account, which deposits it again: one operator compounding its rewards by hand. Each deposit account has its own withdrawal address, and they deposited between May 2023 and July 2025. A further 1,000 validators deposited in November 2020 and since exited belong to the same staker (decision 0075): their principal was sent to one of these deposit accounts and restaked.

The link to Gate. In November 2022 Gate published one of its cold wallets, 0xc882…f071, as holding 140,000 ETH (as reported by TokenInsight); on chain it then held 140,860 ETH. That wallet and 0x0d07…92fe have sent each other about 1.5M ETH in each direction since 2022, and 0x0d07…92fe, which has sent ten million transactions to 1.45 million addresses, is an exchange's hot wallet. It funded three of the five deposit accounts, directly or through one intermediary. The other two were funded mostly from another exchange's hot wallets, as an exchange moving funds between venues would be; they are tied to the fleet by the shared fee recipient and its payments.

Limits. Gate's statement survives only as reported by a third party, and it names the cold wallet rather than the staking addresses. A community-maintained tracker lists five of the withdrawal addresses as Gate's staked ETH; that and explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that 0xc882…f071 or 0x0d07…92fe is not Gate's, or that Gate holds this stake for a single client.
