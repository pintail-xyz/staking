Explanation

The account 0x0c47…84c8 makes deposits for Kraken's staking, restaked through an EigenLayer pod it owns; its validators are attributed to Kraken's staking as allocator. It deposited 355 validators between December 2025 and May 2026, all to its pod; in October 2026, after consolidation, 41 compounding validators were active with about 69k ETH.

Restaking. Every deposit goes to an EigenLayer pod created through EigenLayer's pod manager and owned by this account, so the withdrawals are the account's. Kraken announced in October 2024 that it would restake customers' staked ETH through EigenLayer. Restaking delegates the pod's stake to an EigenLayer operator for additional services without changing who runs the validators. The account delegates to an operator whose own published metadata names it "Kraken [1]", though anyone can delegate to it.

The link to Kraken. Its first gas came in November 2025 from a withdrawal address of Kraken's main deposit account (decision 0007), within the same 40 minutes as the first gas of two other Kraken restaking accounts (decisions 0012 and 0013). Most of its principal, 59,240 ETH, came from an account whose own deposits go to another of Kraken's withdrawal addresses. It deposited through the same batch contract as Kraken's main account, and its pod consolidated its validators in June 2026, the same weeks as Kraken's other accounts.

The batch contract has no caller check, so using it does not by itself show that Kraken sent a deposit; the link rests on the funding. Whether Kraken holds this stake for itself or for a client, Kraken chose who runs the validators. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence that the account deposits keys a client chose.

What would change it

A Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence it deposits keys chosen by a client.
