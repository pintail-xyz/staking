Explanation

The contract 0x5768…867e is Kiln's batch deposit contract (version 2): validators first deposited through it are attributed to Kiln as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. 20,785 validators were deposited through it between March 2024 and October 2026, from 326 sending accounts; about 4,400 were active in October 2026 with some 0.41M ETH, much of it in compounding validators.

Identification. Kiln's documentation names this contract as its V2 batch deposit contract, alongside a deprecated V1 contract (decision 0033). V2 took over from V1: its first deposit was on 27 March 2024 and V1's last on 30 March 2024. The account that created it also created other contracts Kiln operates, including the one behind MetaMask's validator staking.

Why Kiln is the allocator. A client depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider, who generates the keys and runs the validators; the client keeps the withdrawal address. This is the rule applied to all providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005). It holds where the client is itself an integrator or a custodian.

Limits. The contract is open to anyone, so a deposit through it does not by itself prove that Kiln runs the validator. Explorer labels on the contract and its creator are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Kiln statement contradicting it, or evidence that some depositors use the contract with keys Kiln does not run.
