Explanation

The contract 0xe3cb…e8f is the Staking contract of Mantle's mETH Protocol, and validators withdrawing to the address it deposits to are attributed to Mantle as allocator. It deposited 30,681 validators between October 2023 and August 2026; mETH held 1.7% of all stake at its peak in 2024, and about 6,100 validators with 0.19M ETH in October 2026.

Identification. mETH Protocol's documentation names this contract as its Staking contract, and the address its validators withdraw to, 0xd4e1…493f, as its consensus-layer receiver. Every deposit the contract sent uses that withdrawal address, and no validator deposited in any other way withdraws to it. All of the contract's transactions came from a single account.

Why Mantle is the allocator. mETH's documentation on node operators explains that the protocol uses several node operators, whose validators its own service requests and funds. The protocol therefore chooses the operators. No operator is recorded.

Explorer labels on the contracts are recorded as leads. The decision would change if mETH's documentation or contracts showed the Staking contract depositing on others' behalf.

What would change it

mETH's docs or contracts showing the contract deposits on others' behalf.
