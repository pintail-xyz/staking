Explanation

The contract 0x2296…f079 is the Pool Escrow of StakeWise's version 2 staking pool, and validators withdrawing to it are attributed to StakeWise as allocator. 2,751 validators withdrew to it; they made up 0.53% of all stake at their peak in 2021, and all have since exited.

Identification. StakeWise's own list of its mainnet contracts names this contract as its V2 Pool Escrow, together with the pool's Pool and Oracles contracts. The validators were deposited through the Pool contract from February 2021 (912 first deposits, most of them sent by one of its operators of the time) and through the Oracles contract from January 2022 (1,839), all with the Pool Escrow as withdrawal address.

StakeWise chose the operators of its pool, so it is the allocator; no operator is recorded. StakeWise's version 3 vaults are covered by decision 0105. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change if StakeWise's contract list named a different escrow.

What would change it

StakeWise's contract list naming a different escrow.
