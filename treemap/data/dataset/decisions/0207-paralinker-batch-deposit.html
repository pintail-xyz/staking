Explanation

The contract 0xfc3e…c81f is Paralinker's batch deposit contract: validators first deposited through it are attributed to Paralinker as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. It deposited 1,073 validators between December 2022 and July 2025, for seventeen sending accounts, holding as much as 30k ETH in February 2024; 112 were active in October 2026, all previously unattributed.

Identification. Paralinker's repository, a copy of Staked's open batch deposit contract, gives this address as its mainnet deployment. Paralinker's staking guide says it uses batch mode for staking deposits and links that repository; Paralinker's staking is non-custodial, with stakers keeping their withdrawal addresses.

The graffiti. Every one of the 1,069 blocks its validators have proposed is signed "Staking via Paralinker", and nearly all paid the same fee recipient. The contract has no owner and is open to anyone, but the uniform graffiti shows that Paralinker ran all of its validators.

A client depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider (decision 0005). The decision would change with a Paralinker statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys Paralinker does not run are deposited through it.

What would change it

A Paralinker statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys Paralinker does not run are deposited through it.
