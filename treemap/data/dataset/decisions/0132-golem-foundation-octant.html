Explanation

Validators withdrawing to the Safe multisig 0xba19…7ed1 are the Golem Foundation's staking for Octant, its public-goods funding programme, with the Foundation as allocator. 3,138 validators were deposited between June 2023 and February 2024; 3,136 were active in October 2026 with about 100k ETH.

Identification. The Golem Foundation's Octant repository lists this Safe as Octant's mainnet "Withdrawal Target", and the project's backend uses it as the destination of staking proceeds. Octant is funded by the rewards of 100,000 ETH that the Foundation stakes; 100,416 ETH was deposited to the Safe.

On chain. The Safe (three of five signers) was created by one of its owners, who also sent every deposit through the Safe to the deposit contract, with the Safe as withdrawal address. No staking provider's contract was used. The Safe is its validators' own fee recipient, and their graffiti shows client versions only.

Why the Foundation is the allocator. It stakes its own treasury and chose how the stake is run. Whether it runs the nodes itself is not known, so no operator is recorded. Community labels naming the accounts as Octant's and the Foundation's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Golem Foundation statement contradicting it, or evidence that a staking provider chose the validators' operator.

What would change it

A Golem Foundation statement contradicting it, or evidence that a staking provider chose the validators' operator.
