Explanation

The account 0xa858…32ed made the deposits for the staking service of Bitpie, a wallet app: validators withdrawing to the address they moved to are attributed to Bitpie as allocator. It deposited exactly 900 validators, 28,800 ETH, between November and December 2020; they made up 2.4% of all stake at their peak in December 2020, and 148 were active in October 2026. The stake is Bitpie's users'.

What Bitpie published. Bitpie's announcement of November 2022 discloses this account as its ETH 2.0 staking address, with 900 validators and 28,800 ETH. Its guide of March 2021 explains the service: users stake from 0.1 ETH through Bitpie's custodial cloud wallet, Bitpie sets up a validator for every 32 ETH, issues tokens one for one, and keeps 10% of the rewards.

On chain. The account sent the first deposits of exactly 900 validators, all with BLS credentials, later changed to 0x9f4b…481a. Every block these validators have proposed since September 2022 carries graffiti that encodes this account's own address, in the format used for the beacon chain's commemorative tokens, so whoever ran them put Bitpie's address in their graffiti. The account and its withdrawal address were both first funded by the same wallet, and the withdrawal address pays out to many addresses, as Bitpie's wallet paying its users would.

No operator is recorded. Explorer labels and community label sets are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Bitpie statement contradicting it, or evidence that the withdrawal address is not Bitpie's.

What would change it
