Explanation

The contract 0xb3d9…8f39 is the Deposit Manager of Swell's liquid staking token swETH. Validators whose first deposit it sent, and validators withdrawing to it, are attributed to Swell swETH as allocator. It deposited 7,784 validators between April 2023 and October 2025, holding as much as 249k ETH; 350 were active in October 2026, with 11,200 ETH.

Identification on chain. The swETH token contract (name and symbol "swETH") names an access-control manager, and that manager names this contract as its DepositManager and the token as its swETH; the Deposit Manager in turn names the same access-control manager. Swell's published code sets the Deposit Manager's own address as its validators' withdrawal credentials, and every one of the 7,784 validators withdraws to it. All the deposits were sent by one operating account.

Consistency with public information. The active validators' 11,200 ETH, together with the 301 ETH the Deposit Manager holds, is close to the ETH behind swETH's supply (about 12,160 ETH at its exchange rate). DefiLlama has measured Swell's staked value from the same swETH contract since a week after the first deposit. No Swell web page naming the address could be retrieved.

Not covered: Swell's restaking token, rswETH, has its own Deposit Manager, which deposited 4,018 validators in 2024; none is active. The decision would change with a Swell statement contradicting it, or evidence that the swETH contracts are not Swell's.

What would change it

A Swell statement contradicting it, or evidence that the swETH contracts are not Swell's.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
