Explanation

The address 0xeee2…e4c8 held Celsius's staking from 2021 until its validators exited in 2023 and early 2024: validators withdrawing to it are attributed to Celsius as allocator. 4,943 validators deposited directly by it from August 2021 to April 2022 remain attributed here, peaking at 158k ETH in May 2022. The 6,294 it deposited later through Blockdaemon's batch contract are attributed to Blockdaemon as allocator (decision 0041).

The court record. The examiner's report in Celsius's bankruptcy (Doc. 1956) cites Celsius's token CEL by its contract address and lists direct staking among Celsius's deployments. It names no wallet.

The link through CEL. CEL's first transaction makes one account its owner, and the deployer then sent that account the whole supply of 700 million CEL; only the issuer would hold that. A wallet that paid the owner's gas in 2021 and sent it CEL trades both ETH and CEL with a hub that, together with a custody wallet, moved most of CEL's supply, as only Celsius would. That hub supplies a trading wallet which sent 0xeee2…e4c8 its first 32 ETH on the day of its first deposit in August 2021. Every material payment into the address came from this same network, and after its validators exited it sent its ETH back into it: 161k ETH to the custody wallet and 205k ETH to the wallet that had paid the owner's gas.

The address was the sender and the withdrawal address of all its deposits. Explorer labels on the wallets involved are recorded as leads, not relied on. Celsius's other staking account, which deposited only through Figment's contracts, is attributed to Figment under decisions 0029 and 0031. The decision would change with evidence that this address staked for someone other than Celsius, that the CEL owner's wallets are not Celsius's, or that a provider chose the operators.
