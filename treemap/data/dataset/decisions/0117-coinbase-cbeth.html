Anchor · decided 2026-10-20

This contract is Coinbase's cbETH: a token for ETH staked with Coinbase, valued at the rate it publishes.

Grounds

  • First partyCoinbase's own developer code (AgentKit) lists this as cbETH's Ethereum mainnet contract
  • On chainNearly all of its 2.69M minted cbETH went to one account, which passed it on to a single address
  • Third partyPrisma Risk's cbETH assessment lists the same contract

Explanation

The contract 0xbe98…9704 is Coinbase's cbETH (Coinbase Wrapped Staked ETH): a token for ETH staked with Coinbase, valued at an exchange rate Coinbase publishes. The decision is used by the "Forms of ETH" page, which counts cbETH's supply at its exchange rate as Coinbase's issue; the wallet receiving its minted tokens also helps identify Coinbase's staking (decisions 0016, 0017 and 0020).

Identification. Coinbase's own developer code (its AgentKit token list) gives this as cbETH's Ethereum mainnet contract, and Prisma Risk's assessment of cbETH lists the same contract.

On chain. Every cbETH ever minted, 2,691,301 tokens, went to four accounts, and one of them received all but test amounts. That account passed its cbETH on to a single address, 0xa9d1…3e43: every transfer up to late 2022 did so, as did every one of a sample spread across all its later transactions. cbETH's supply peaked at 1.47M in May 2024 and has been flat at 393,751 since March 2025.

cbETH's backing is held by Coinbase, outside the execution layer, so nothing is netted out of ETH for it. An explorer label on the receiving address is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with Coinbase naming another contract as cbETH, or the minted cbETH going somewhere other than Coinbase.

What would change it

Coinbase naming another contract as cbETH, or the minted cbETH going somewhere other than Coinbase.

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
