Explanation

The validators withdrawing to four Safe multisigs, 0x2456…1ba8, 0x9225…a589, 0x3b83…741c and 0x76a7…d94a, are attributed to Attestant as allocator. They hold 16 validators and about 32,100 ETH (November 2026). The depositor is not identified, so Attestant, the firm it chose, is the allocator.

One depositor. A single account deposited all 1,001 of the Safes' original validators between May and August 2024, about 250 for each Safe, and sent nothing else; its ETH came from another Safe. The four Safes have acted together since: on 8 November 2024 each sent ETH to the same Safe within ten minutes, and between 24 and 27 March 2026 each consolidated its validators into four.

Attestant's fleet. Their validators sign the client builds they run (Nethermind and Prysm) and moved through the same eight builds together. Two of the Safes' moves are timed to within a day, three times each, and each falls in a switch made by the Lido validators that Attestant runs, which upgrade on their own schedule, days after a build appears (method graffiti.upgrade_lockstep). From May 2026 the four Safes' validators build nine in ten of their blocks locally, with blank or Besu extra data, as Attestant's Lido validators do, and as does the share of the iShares Staked Ethereum Trust that its prospectus and its deposits show Attestant runs (0329).

The decision would change with evidence that another firm runs these validators, or if their behaviour diverged from Attestant's fleet.

What would change it

Evidence that another firm runs these validators, or their behaviour diverging from Attestant's fleet.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.

  • blocks 21,140,109 to 21,140,159 (8 November 2024) — Each of the four Safes sent ETH to the same Safe, 0x13f073bb6338036649e16e256f31784666a8fbbb, within ten minutes.
  • 250 consolidation requests from each Safe, 24 to 27 March 2026 — Each Safe consolidated its validators into four.
  • client codes NM…P… (Nethermind with Prysm builds) from June 2026 — All four Safes' validators moved through the same eight builds over the same days; 0x3b83… and 0x9225… each matched three of the switches of the Lido validators Attestant runs to within a day (upgrade lockstep), with none against.
  • their blocks since January 2026 — Until April 2026 almost all were bought from MEV-Boost relays; from May 2026 nine in ten were built locally, with blank or Besu extra data, as are those of the Lido validators Attestant runs and of the share of the iShares Staked Ethereum Trust Attestant runs (0329).
