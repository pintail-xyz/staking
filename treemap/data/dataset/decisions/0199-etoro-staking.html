Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x1135…de29 are attributed to the ETH staking of eToro, the trading platform, with eToro as allocator. The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive: eToro names no address, and there is no check of the stake's size against eToro's accounts. 2,482 validators were deposited from July 2024; 2,322 were active in October 2026 with about 74k ETH.

What eToro says. eToro's annual report on Form 20-F for 2025 says it stakes ETH using staking validators that it controls, keeping a portion of the rewards and passing the rest to users. Press reports of eToro's announcement of 31 July 2024 (FX News Group, Finance Magnates) date the launch of ETH staking to that day, say it requires users to opt in, and describe monthly reward updates.

The timing. One account made every deposit since July 2024, all directly to the deposit contract. After four isolated deposits earlier in July, 46 validators were deposited on 29 July 2024, 135 on 30 July and 117 on 31 July, the day of the launch. Later bursts match no announcement found; since staking is opt-in they may be waves of users opting in.

The pattern. The depositing account's ETH came only from two wallets in turn, both supplied by a third. About once a month, nearly always between the 2nd and the 13th, the withdrawal address sends most of the month's rewards back to the current funding wallet, consistent with monthly reward updates. Graffiti is blank or carries client versions, and fee recipients are block builders, so no operator identifies itself; eToro's report says it controls the validators.

Searches of court records, SEC filings and eToro's own former token contracts found no eToro address that would anchor the attribution more firmly. Explorer labels naming the funding wallets as eToro's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a statement or address list from eToro that includes or excludes this address, or evidence that the wallets funding it belong to someone else.
