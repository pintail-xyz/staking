Explanation

The contract 0xb61e…dc78 administers Kelp DAO's contracts. Validators withdrawing to an EigenLayer pod owned by one of the contracts it administers are attributed to Kelp's restaking token rsETH, with Kelp as allocator. 10,096 deposits, about 323k ETH, went to those pods between February 2024 and December 2025; about 4,500 validators were active in October 2026 with some 0.14M ETH.

Identification. Kelp DAO's repository lists this contract as the administrator of its contracts, owned by Kelp's timelock, together with its proxy factory, deposit pool, fee receiver and NodeDelegator contracts. On chain, five contracts administered by it own EigenLayer pods, all created by Kelp's proxy factory; four of them are NodeDelegators listed in the repository, and the fifth, built on another implementation, holds no stake now. Almost all deposits to the pods were sent through the delegators themselves, and the validators' blocks pay Kelp's fee receiver or deposit pool.

Why Kelp is the allocator. Kelp chooses the node operators for each delegator; its repository's notes name them, several running SSV clusters. No operator is recorded here. Before this decision these validators had been taken to be their operators' own, on the strength of an occasional block signed with an operator's name; the withdrawal side now identifies Kelp directly.

The decision would change with a Kelp statement contradicting it, or a pod owner under this administrator that is not Kelp's.

What would change it

A Kelp statement contradicting it, or a pod owner under this ProxyAdmin that is not Kelp's.
