Six addresses that proved common control together with BitMine's wallets deposited exactly the 159,728 ETH by which BitMine's reported staked ETH rose in the week of 20 October 2026.

AttributionAllocator BitMine treasury staking · decided 2026-10-27

Validators withdrawing to these 6 addresses are BitMine's treasury staking.

Grounds

  • On chainAll six sent 0.001 ETH in the same window on 31 August 2026 as all of BitMine's wallets in decision 0087, to the same new account
  • On chainOn 20 October 2026 they deposited 159,728 ETH; no other BitMine address deposited in October
  • First partyBitMine's reported staked ETH rose from 5,067,309 (18 October) to 5,227,037 (25 October): by exactly 159,728
  • On chainLike BitMine's other wallets, each deposits compounding validators to itself, through the same batch contracts

Explanation

Validators withdrawing to these six addresses are BitMine Immersion Technologies' treasury staking, with BitMine as allocator. On 20 October 2026 the six deposited 159,728 ETH in 84 compounding (0x02) validators of about 1,900 ETH, each address depositing to itself. In late October 2026 the deposits were still waiting to be processed by the beacon chain, so the validators are attributed as they appear.

Common control. All six took part in the control transfers of 31 August 2026 described under decision 0087: each sent 0.001 ETH to the same newly created account as BitMine's other wallets, within the same 73 minutes. Until 20 October none of them had staked; they were among the senders that held unstaked ETH.

The match with BitMine's figures. BitMine reported 5,067,309 ETH staked as of 11 October and again as of 18 October 2026, and 5,227,037 ETH as of 25 October 2026. The rise, 159,728 ETH, equals these six addresses' deposits to the ETH, and no address of decisions 0087, 0115 or 0138 deposited during October. BitMine therefore counts ETH as staked from the time it is deposited, before the validators are active.

The same way of staking. Each address deposits compounding validators to itself, as BitMine's other wallets do: five of them through the two batch contracts that BitMine's other wallets also used, and one directly to the deposit contract.

Limits. As for decision 0087, the control transfers show one holder, or parties acting together, and the identification with BitMine rests on its reported figures; an exact match to the ETH makes coincidence unlikely. No operator has been identified for these validators. The decision would change with a later BitMine figure these deposits do not fit, or with evidence that an address is held by someone other than BitMine.

What would change it

A later BitMine figure these deposits do not fit, or evidence that an address is held by someone other than BitMine.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
