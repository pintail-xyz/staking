Explanation

The account 0xd57d…194e made HTX's staking deposits from May 2023: validators withdrawing to the addresses it used are attributed to HTX (formerly Huobi) as allocator. It sent 9,388 deposits, about 300k ETH, between May 2023 and April 2025; in October 2026 about 2,650 of its validators were active with some 85k ETH. HTX's customers own the stake.

HTX's reserve wallets. HTX's proof-of-reserves snapshots of December 2022 and May 2023 list reserve addresses, each with a signature from the address's own key over a fixed message, which proves that HTX controls it.

The funding. The account received about 270k ETH, most of it from four multisig wallets. Three of the signed reserve wallets sent 66,082 ETH to two of those multisigs. The key that created the withdrawal wallets through a multisig wallet factory was itself first funded by two signed reserve wallets, and it also paid gas to the signers of two of the funding multisigs and sent the deposit account 3 ETH. The signers of the funding multisigs overlap with those of the withdrawal wallets. In December 2024 one withdrawal wallet's ETH, 56,839 ETH, passed through a Safe multisig back to this account, which deposited it again with the Safe as withdrawal address.

The deposits went through a public batch deposit contract that anyone can use, which is not itself evidence. Some 2024 funding came from other exchanges' hot wallets, as exchanges move funds between venues. Several operators sign some of these validators' blocks, mostly in SSV clusters; they remain operators only, with HTX as allocator. An explorer label is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with an HTX statement contradicting it, or evidence that the funding multisigs or the creating key belong to someone else.

What would change it

An HTX statement contradicting it, or evidence that the funding multisigs or the creating key belong to someone else.
