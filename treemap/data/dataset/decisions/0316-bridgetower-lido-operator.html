Lido operator 1/17 was Bridgetower's until Solstice acquired its staking division in December 2024.

Operator or ownerOperated by Bridgetower from 2022-01-14, for Solstice · decided 2026-10-29

Lido's node operator 1/17 is run by Bridgetower from its registration on 14 January 2022.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's registry added operator 1/17 as "BridgeTower" on 14 January 2022
  • First partySolstice Staking announced on 16 December 2024 that it had acquired Bridgetower's staking division

Explanation

Lido's node operator registry, read on chain, added operator 1/17 as "BridgeTower" on 14 January 2022. It is recorded as run by Bridgetower from that date until Solstice Staking announced on 16 December 2024 that it had acquired Bridgetower Capital's staking division, including its team, after which it is shown as Solstice's (decision 0304). The registry renamed the operator "Solstice" on 31 January 2025.

What would change it

Evidence that operator 1/17 was run by a company other than Bridgetower before December 2024.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
