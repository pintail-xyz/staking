Explanation

The contract 0x8103…3096 is the LiquidStaking contract of NodeDAO's liquid staking token nETH. Validators whose first deposit it sent, and those withdrawing to the addresses those deposits name, are attributed to NodeDAO nETH as allocator. It sent 508 deposits between March 2023 and March 2024, holding as much as 15k ETH; none is active now.

Identification. NodeDAO's repository lists its Ethereum contracts: a timelock controller, this LiquidStaking proxy and a ConsensusVault proxy. On chain, the timelock owns both proxies. 506 of the 508 deposits name the ConsensusVault as withdrawal address; the other two each name an ordinary account, for reasons not established, but NodeDAO's contract sent them.

NodeDAO chooses the operators of its pool, so it is the allocator. No operator is recorded. The decision would change with a NodeDAO statement contradicting it, or evidence that the listed contracts are not NodeDAO's.

What would change it

A NodeDAO statement contradicting it, or evidence that the listed contracts are not NodeDAO's.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
