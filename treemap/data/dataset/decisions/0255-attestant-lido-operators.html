Lido operators 1/29 and 4/0, and Lido's operator group 4.1, are Attestant's.

Operator or ownerOperated by Attestant from 2022-12-16, for Attestant (BVI) Limited, Attestant - PGO, Attestant (BVI) Limited · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's node operators 1/29 (Attestant (BVI) Limited) and 4/0 (Attestant - PGO), and Lido's operator group 4.1, are run by Attestant.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's registry added operator 1/29 as "Attestant (BVI) Limited" on 16 December 2022
  • On chainOperator 4/0 named itself "Attestant" in July 2026, then "Attestant - PGO"
  • On chainLido's metadata registry names operator group 4.1 "Attestant (BVI) Limited"

Explanation

Lido's node operator registry, read on chain, added operator 29 of the curated module as "Attestant (BVI) Limited" on 16 December 2022. In the second curated module, operator 0 set its name to "Attestant" on 24 July 2026 and to "Attestant - PGO" on 4 August 2026, and Lido's operator metadata registry groups Attestant's operators as group 4.1, "Attestant (BVI) Limited". These operators are recorded as run by Attestant from the first registration. Who owns Attestant is a separate decision (0259).

What would change it

A Lido or Attestant statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.

  • 0x05ffd939…36903aab — Lido adds operator 1/29, "Attestant (BVI) Limited"
  • 0xb656f3d0…8165f2be — Operator 4/0 sets its name to "Attestant - PGO"
