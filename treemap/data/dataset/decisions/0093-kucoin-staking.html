Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x9f4c…2a2a, and those deposited by four accounts linked to KuCoin's wallets, are KuCoin's staking, with the exchange as allocator. 927 validators withdraw to that address; 625 were active in October 2026 with 20,000 ETH. The stake is KuCoin's customers'.

KuCoin's own list. KuCoin's post "Transparency and Trust" of 11 November 2022 lists its hot and cold wallets at a snapshot of that day and says KuCoin was then staking 29,696 ETH, without listing the validators. It names two of the four accounts that deposited these validators. The other two were first funded by wallets on the list.

The match. The four accounts deposited all 927 validators between November 2020 and October 2022, before the snapshot: 29,664 ETH, one validator short of the figure KuCoin gave. No other listed address sent deposits or is a withdrawal address.

No operator is recorded. A community-compiled reserve list and explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that KuCoin holds these validators for a client, or a KuCoin statement contradicting its list.

What would change it
