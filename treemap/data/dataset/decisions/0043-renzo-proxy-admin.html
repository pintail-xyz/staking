Explanation

The contract 0x9162…1982 administers Renzo's contracts. Validators withdrawing to an EigenLayer pod owned by one of the contracts it administers are attributed to Renzo's restaking (ezETH) as allocator. 42,890 validators have withdrawn to such pods, first activated in December 2023; by October 2026 only about 550 remained active, Renzo having withdrawn almost all of its native restaking.

Identification. Renzo's documentation lists its RestakeManager contract. The RestakeManager's EIP-1967 administrator slot holds 0x9162…1982. That administrator controls five contracts which own EigenLayer pods; all five share one implementation and each stores the RestakeManager's address. Four were created by Renzo's deployment account and the fifth by another account. All the validators withdrawing to their pods were deposited through a proxy contract that Renzo's deployment account created.

Why Renzo is the allocator. Renzo chooses the node operators that run its validators and the EigenLayer operators its delegators delegate to. No operator is recorded. No validator here has a conflicting allocator claim.

No source names the administrator itself, so the link rests on chain data from the documented RestakeManager. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Renzo statement contradicting it, or a pod owner under this administrator that is not Renzo's.

What would change it

A Renzo statement contradicting it, or a pod owner under it that is not Renzo's.
