Explanation

These nine withdrawal addresses hold Bitcoin Suisse's staking since 2023, continuing its staking from 2020 (decision 0023). 17,449 validators were deposited to them between May 2023 and August 2026; about 10,500 were active in October 2026 with some 0.34M ETH. Eight of the addresses hold exactly 2,000 validators each, and the ninth was still filling. Bitcoin Suisse is the allocator.

The graffiti. Every block proposed by a validator that has ever withdrawn to one of these addresses carries graffiti beginning "bitcoinsuisse.com", alone or followed by client codes: 66,061 blocks by 14,993 validators, with no other graffiti at all.

The link to Bitcoin Suisse's 2020 staking. All nine addresses sweep their withdrawals to the same wallet, 0x7b45…fcb1, as the 2020 addresses do. In one block on 20 October 2023, two of these addresses swept to it together with five of the 2020 addresses: one operator runs both. Some sweeps go instead to an account first funded by that wallet, which itself sent 1,644 of these deposits.

The deposits. All went directly to the deposit contract, from 287 sending accounts. Many were funded by accounts already linked to Bitcoin Suisse; others look like clients' own accounts. Whoever funded a deposit, the validator's withdrawals go to Bitcoin Suisse's wallet, so Bitcoin Suisse holds the stake and set up the validators. Thirteen other small groups of validators deposited alongside these, each withdrawing to its sender's own address, are left out, since they may be clients' own.

Limits. Graffiti is set by whoever runs a validator, and anyone could type a name. A fleet of this size signing Bitcoin Suisse's own domain on every block is taken to be Bitcoin Suisse's, or run for it under its name; no operator is recorded. Explorer labels on the accounts are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Bitcoin Suisse statement contradicting it, or evidence that a client chose its own operator for some of these validators.

What would change it

A Bitcoin Suisse statement contradicting it, or evidence a client chose its own operator for some of these validators.
