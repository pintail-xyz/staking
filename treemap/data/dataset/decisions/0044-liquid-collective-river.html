Explanation

The contract 0x8c1b…6549 is River, the contract behind Liquid Collective's liquid staking token LsETH. Validators withdrawing to the address River deposits to are attributed to Liquid Collective as allocator. 16,191 validators have withdrawn there, first activated in November 2022; about 8,500 were active in October 2026 with some 0.27M ETH.

Identification. Liquid Collective's documentation names this contract as River, its LsETH contract. Between July 2023 and October 2026 River sent 16,126 deposits, all to the withdrawal address 0x0afd…5154, which is the withdrawal credential River itself reports. A further 81 validators withdrawing to the same address were deposited in 2022 and 2023, before River's own deposits began, through the Safe multisig 0xde55…6b62; since they withdraw to Liquid Collective's address, they are Liquid Collective's too. No validator here has a conflicting allocator claim.

Why Liquid Collective is the allocator. River deposits with keys supplied by the node operators Liquid Collective registers, so the protocol chooses the operators. No operator is recorded, because River's operator registry is not read.

Explorer labels on the contracts are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Liquid Collective statement contradicting it.

What would change it

A Liquid Collective statement contradicting it.
