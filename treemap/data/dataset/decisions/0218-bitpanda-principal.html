Explanation

Bitpanda, the European exchange, is the principal of the validators withdrawing to these 19 addresses: the ETH is Bitpanda's, held for its customers, and Bitpanda chose the providers that run it. Validators deposited through Kiln's or Figment's contracts keep those providers as allocators. In October 2026 the addresses held 274 active validators with about 0.28M ETH, many of them compounding; the earliest three held about 200k ETH in early 2024.

The link through Bitpanda's token. Bitpanda gives the contract address of BEST, its exchange token. On chain, the whole supply of one billion BEST passed at creation to one account, and in 2023 313 million BEST, nearly a third of the supply, passed from there to 0xf197…, which in turn sent large amounts to 0xb10e… and 0xb8cb…. Only the issuer holds a third of its own token's supply. Bitpanda announced a burn of BEST each month until BEST was merged into its Vision token in 2025 (Vision token page). Bitpanda's own table of burns is no longer published, so the amounts were checked against an independent copy of the table. From October 2022 to June 2024, 21 burns came from 0xb8cb…, and from July 2024 nine came from 0x0529…, each matching the published amount and date.

The stake. 0xb8cb…, 0xf197… and 0xb10e… deposited 6,269 validators between March 2023 and February 2024 through Kiln's first batch deposit contract. This matches Bitpanda's launch of ETH staking in May 2023, which Kiln announced it was powering and describes in a customer story. Most of those validators exited in mid-2025. Since 2025 these accounts and 0x0529…, which also pays out to many customers as an exchange hot wallet does, have funded fresh addresses with 15,000 to 40,000 ETH each; each then deposited its own validators through Kiln's or Figment's contract.

Limits. Bitpanda does not name these addresses; the link runs through its token's treasury and burns. Community label sets naming the accounts as Bitpanda's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that these addresses hold a Bitpanda client's ETH, or that BEST's treasury is held by a party other than Bitpanda.

What would change it

Evidence that these addresses hold a Bitpanda client's ETH, or that BEST's treasury is held by a party other than Bitpanda.
