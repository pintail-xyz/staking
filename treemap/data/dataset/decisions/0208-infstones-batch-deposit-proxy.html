Explanation

The contract 0x85a5…42c9 is InfStones' successor to its batch deposit contract for the imToken wallet (decision 0189): validators first deposited through it are attributed to InfStones as allocator and operator, while the stakers keep their own withdrawal addresses. It deposited 152 validators between June and October 2026; 128 were active in October 2026 with about 5,900 ETH.

Identification. No document names this contract; it rests on its ownership. InfStones' key, the owner of the imToken contract identified in decision 0189 (which InfStones and imToken announced), created this contract and its implementation, and is its owner and proxy administrator.

The handover. The older contract's last deposit was on 24 June 2026, and this one's first on 27 June. Six of its 21 senders used both, and four of its withdrawal addresses already held validators deposited through the older contract. Some of its validators' blocks pay the Safe that replaced the fee recipient of InfStones' SSV operators.

This is weaker than decision 0189's match with imToken's published figures, but the ownership is direct. The decision would change with an InfStones or imToken statement that the contract is not theirs, or evidence that keys InfStones does not run are deposited through it.

What would change it
