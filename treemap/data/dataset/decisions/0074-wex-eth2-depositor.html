Explanation

The account 0xbb84…86db made deposits for the staking of the former funds of WEX, the exchange that succeeded BTC-e and closed in 2018. Validators withdrawing to the address they moved to are attributed to "WEX (former funds; holder unknown)" as allocator: whoever controlled WEX's former wallets in 2020. 650 validators withdraw there, all active in October 2026; they made up 2.4% of all stake at the beacon chain's launch.

The 2017 wallets. ForkLog reported in October 2018 that 485,705 ETH left an address that had belonged to BTC-e on 29 July 2017, and that 480,000 ETH more went in 32 transactions, from September 2017 to January 2018, to another WEX wallet. The chain matches exactly: on 29 July 2017 one exchange hot wallet sent 485,705.46 ETH to another address, its last outgoing transaction; that address then sent 480,000 ETH in 33 transfers between September 2017 and January 2018 to a further exchange hot wallet.

The deposits. In November 2020 the account received 15,514 ETH from 95 wallets, many in a single block, and deposited 500 validators with BLS credentials, later changed to 0xf4d1…63c5; that address itself deposited 150 more in 2023. 80% of the account's ETH came from wallets funded out of the 2017 WEX wallets' outflows, most of them funded in September 2017 and then dormant until 2020. Its first funder received its only ETH from WEX's hot wallet in September 2017.

Limits. The identification rests on news reports from 2018, which give amounts and dates but no addresses; the chain matches them exactly. WEX had closed two years before the deposits, so who held the funds in 2020 is unknown. Explorer labels and community label sets are recorded as leads. No operator is recorded. The decision would change with evidence of who held WEX's ETH in 2020, or that these wallets passed to someone unconnected with WEX.

What would change it

Evidence of who held WEX's ETH in 2020, or that these wallets passed to someone unconnected with WEX.
