Explanation

The account 0x194b…9d5b2 made HTX's (formerly Huobi) first staking deposits, and validators withdrawing to the address they moved to are attributed to HTX as allocator. It deposited 1,243 validators in December 2020; together with HTX's second deposit account (decision 0073), its validators held 2.8% of all stake at their peak in early 2021. All have since exited. HTX's customers owned the stake.

HTX's reserve wallets. HTX's proof-of-reserves snapshots of December 2022 and May 2023 list reserve addresses, each with a signature from the address's own key over a fixed message, which proves that HTX controls it.

The funding. The account received 40,000 ETH in ten transfers in December 2020. 15,000 ETH came from 0xcac7…c3fc, one of the signed reserve wallets, and another 15,000 from two high-volume hot wallets that keep that reserve wallet supplied (390,000 ETH between them).

The withdrawal address. All the validators were deposited with BLS credentials and later changed to 0x08de…375d. That address's first transaction was 0.1 ETH from 0xa5d7…951e, another signed reserve wallet, in April 2023; it has since sent its withdrawals on, and part of them back to HTX's other deposit account to be restaked.

Explorer labels on the accounts are recorded as leads. No operator is recorded. The decision would change with an HTX statement contradicting it.

What would change it
