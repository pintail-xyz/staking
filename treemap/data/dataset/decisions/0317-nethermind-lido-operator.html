Lido operator 1/25 was Nethermind's until its validators passed to Twinstake in October 2025.

Operator or ownerOperated by Nethermind from 2022-06-24, for Twinstake · decided 2026-10-29

Lido's node operator 1/25 is run by Nethermind from its registration on 24 June 2022.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's registry added operator 1/25 as "Nethermind" on 24 June 2022
  • On chainNethermind and Twinstake proposed moving its validators to Twinstake; the registry renamed it Twinstake on 2 October 2025

Explanation

Lido's node operator registry, read on chain, added operator 1/25 as "Nethermind" on 24 June 2022. Nethermind and Twinstake, a staking provider Nethermind founded as a joint venture, proposed in July 2025 moving the operator's validators to Twinstake's infrastructure; after Lido's vote, the registry renamed the operator "Twinstake" on 2 October 2025. It is recorded as run by Nethermind until that date and by Twinstake from it (decision 0309).

What would change it

Evidence that operator 1/25 was run by a company other than Nethermind before October 2025.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.

  • 0xd08aa7c3…a62cf851 — Lido adds operator 1/25 as "Nethermind"
  • 0xfff774d5…1bfff2ad — Lido renames operator 1/25 "Twinstake"
