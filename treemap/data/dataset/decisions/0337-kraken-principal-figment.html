Explanation

Kraken is the principal of the validators withdrawing to its deposit accounts 0x39ed…82e5 and 0xe1fc…8267 (decisions 0335 and 0336): the ETH was Kraken's or its customers', and Kraken chose how it was staked.

Who allocated them. Both accounts deposited through Figment's depositor contract, so Figment is taken to have run the validators, and as the provider the stake was handed to, it is their allocator. From 19 March 2025, Kraken Staking (formerly Staked), which Kraken owns, registered 3,161 of them on SSV on its own operators, taking over their keys; from then on Kraken is their allocator. The other 2,581, most of them exited before then, stay Figment's as allocator, with Kraken as principal.

The decision would change if either account were found not to be Kraken's, or with evidence that Kraken staked through them for a client who chose Figment.

What would change it

Decisions 0335 or 0336 being reverted, or evidence that Kraken staked through these accounts for a client who chose Figment.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
