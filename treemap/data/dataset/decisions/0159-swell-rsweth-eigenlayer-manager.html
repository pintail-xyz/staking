Explanation

The contract 0xc94c…0936 is the EigenLayer manager of Swell's restaking token rswETH. Validators whose first deposit it sent, and those withdrawing to the EigenLayer pods of its staker proxies, are attributed to Swell rswETH as allocator. It deposited 3,856 validators from August 2024, holding as much as 26k ETH.

Identification on chain. rswETH's Deposit Manager (decision 0158) names this contract as its EigenLayer manager, and the manager shares the rswETH token's access-control manager. Swell's published code has the manager stake validators through staker proxies, each with its own EigenLayer pod; on chain it has 40 such proxies, each owning a pod, and each proxy names the Deposit Manager as its own. Every validator it deposited withdraws to one of those pods.

Consistency with public information. DefiLlama has measured Swell's restaking value from the rswETH contract since the day after its first deposit. rswETH's supply exceeds the ETH staked by these validators; rswETH also accepts other liquid staking tokens, restaked in EigenLayer directly, which would account for the difference.

The decision would change with a Swell statement contradicting it, or evidence that the rswETH contracts are not Swell's.
