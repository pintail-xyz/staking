Explanation

Crypto.com is the principal of the validators withdrawing to its staking wallet 0x93db…bc8b: the ETH is Crypto.com's, staked for its customers, and Crypto.com chose how it is staked. In October 2026 about 2,230 such validators were active, with some 78k ETH.

The wallet sent all its own deposits and is its validators' withdrawal address, so whoever holds it chose each staking route. Some deposits went through providers' contracts, of Figment, Kiln and P2P.org; those validators keep the provider as their allocator, and this decision names the client that hired it. The others it staked directly.

Why the wallet is Crypto.com's. Decision 0183 sets out the link: the wallet is tied to the account that funds the keys of Crypto.com's staked-ETH token, CDCETH. That token's contract appears in Crypto.com's audit of its wrapped tokens and in a proposal co-authored by Crypto.com to list CDCETH on Aave, and Crypto.com's own bug bounty programme lists it.

Crypto.com's customers are the ultimate owners of the stake; the principal recorded here is the account holder that chose the providers. The decision would change with evidence that the wallet holds stake for someone other than Crypto.com, or that the account it is tied to is not Crypto.com's.

What would change it

Evidence that 0x93db holds stake for someone other than Crypto.com, or that 0xcffa is not Crypto.com's.
