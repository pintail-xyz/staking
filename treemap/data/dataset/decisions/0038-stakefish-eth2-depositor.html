Explanation

The contract 0x0194…d801 is stakefish's batch deposit contract: validators first deposited through it are attributed to stakefish as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. 25,334 validators were deposited through it between November 2020 and October 2026, from 1,278 sending accounts; about 4,100 were active in October 2026 with some 0.30M ETH.

The code. The contract's verified source is identical, once comments and blank lines are removed, to the batch deposit contract in stakefish's own repository as it stood on 18 November 2020, five days before the contract was created. stakefish announced the completion of its audit before launch. The contract charges a service fee for each validator, paid to the provider.

The graffiti. Of the 21,061 validators deposited through it that have proposed a block, 20,773 have signed graffiti naming stakefish ("Stake Earn Relax 🐠 stakefish" or similar). 279 signed only other graffiti and nine only blank.

Limits. Anyone can deploy a copy of published code, and anyone paying the fee can deposit through the contract, so neither the code nor the route alone shows that stakefish runs a validator. With almost every validator signing stakefish's name, the contract is taken to be stakefish's. The 279 validators signing other graffiti are probably clients of other operators using the contract; they remain with stakefish here. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a stakefish statement contradicting it, or evidence that more depositors use the contract with keys stakefish does not run.

What would change it

A stakefish statement contradicting it, or evidence some depositors use it with keys stakefish does not run.
