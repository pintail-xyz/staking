Explanation

The account 0x4462…e86c made deposits for Kraken's staking, restaked through an EigenLayer pod it owns; its validators are attributed to Kraken's staking as allocator. It deposited 3,802 validators between May and September 2025, holding as much as 122k ETH; by October 2026 they had been consolidated into other validators.

Restaking. Every deposit went to an EigenLayer pod created through EigenLayer's pod manager and owned by this account, so the withdrawals are the account's. Kraken announced in October 2024 that it would restake customers' staked ETH through EigenLayer, so pods owned by Kraken's accounts are expected. Restaking delegates the pod's stake to an EigenLayer operator for additional services; it does not change who runs the validators. The account delegates to an operator whose own published metadata names it "Kraken [2]".

The link to Kraken. Its first gas came on 21 March 2025 from 0x210b…2a1b, a withdrawal address of Kraken's main deposit account (decision 0007), within the same 40 minutes as the first gas of two other deposit accounts (decisions 0008 and 0009). Another of Kraken's withdrawal addresses then sent it 23 ETH just before its first deposit and 107,000 ETH before its large batches of June 2025. It deposited through the same batch contract as Kraken's main account, later paid the first gas of a further Kraken deposit account (decision 0012), and its pod consolidated its validators in June 2026, the same weeks as Kraken's other accounts.

Limits. This decision is the reference point for several others, and it is itself weak. The restaking announcement is reported by a third party, and anyone can delegate to an EigenLayer operator, so the operator's name shows only that the account chose a Kraken-named operator. The funding from Kraken's withdrawal addresses is the stronger link. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence that the account deposits keys a client chose.

What would change it

A Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence it deposits keys chosen by a client.
