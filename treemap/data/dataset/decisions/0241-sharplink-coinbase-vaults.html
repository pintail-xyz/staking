Explanation

Sharplink, Inc. is the principal of the validators withdrawing to these 88 addresses. 86 of them are vaults in Coinbase's custody staking, most staking 3,200 ETH in 100 validators; the other two collect the vaults' rewards and stake them again. In October 2026 the addresses held about 9,000 active validators with some 296k ETH. Coinbase remains the custodian of the vaults and, by decisions 0134 and 0242, their allocator; this decision records whose stake it is.

The link to Sharplink. Decision 0240 identifies a group of Sharplink's wallets, one of which funded seven Coinbase vaults of 3,200 ETH in August 2025. Those seven vaults pay their consensus rewards to the same two wallets, 0xf036…51e2 until October 2025 and 0x42c5…02bb since, as 86 further vaults do. The two wallets stake what they collect in validators of their own, through Coinbase's batch deposit contract. The vaults' validators also name one fee recipient, 0xd6a7…4a61, for their execution-layer rewards: it appears in 316 blocks from August 2025 to October 2026, every one proposed by a validator of these vaults, and it too passes its balance to the reward wallets. A custodian's settings of this kind are per client, so the 93 vaults are taken to belong to one client, Sharplink.

The match with Sharplink's figures. The vaults received 308k ETH of deposits between 4 August and 7 September 2025, the weeks in which Sharplink bought most of its ETH: its holdings reached 740,760 ETH on 17 August and 797,704 on 24 August. Sharplink reports its native ETH, the part not held as LsETH or weETH. By 19 October 2025 these vaults and the wallets of decision 0240 had deposited 600,114 ETH to validators; Sharplink reported 601,143 native ETH and 258,710 ETH in LsETH that day. By 31 March 2026 the deposits were 607,615 ETH against 589,305 native ETH; some validators of decision 0240 had exited by then.

Limits. No document names these addresses. The identification rests on the shared reward and fee settings, which tie these vaults to the seven funded by Sharplink's wallet, and on the match with Sharplink's figures; it would fail if Coinbase used one fee recipient and one reward wallet for several clients. The decision would change with a Sharplink disclosure these deposits do not fit, or with evidence that the fee recipient or the reward wallets serve another client.

What would change it

A Sharplink disclosure these deposits do not fit, or evidence that the fee recipient or the reward wallets serve another client.

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
