Explanation

The contract 0x84db…6670 is Ankr's liquid staking pool, which made the deposits for Ankr's ETH liquid staking: validators withdrawing to the withdrawal pool it names are attributed to Ankr as allocator. 1,840 validators were deposited through it between November 2020 and November 2025; they made up 1.4% of all stake at their peak in early 2021, and 261 were active in October 2026.

Identification. Ankr's developer documentation names this contract as its mainnet GlobalPool, the contract users stake ETH through, and its page on liquid staking mechanics names 0x6742…9348 as the withdrawal address for Ankr's validators. Every validator deposited through the pool either names that withdrawal address or was later changed to it from BLS credentials.

Ankr chooses the operators of its pool's validators, so it is the allocator. No operator is recorded. Explorer labels and community label sets are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that the contract deposited for others.

What would change it

Evidence the contract deposited for others, or that the tag is wrong.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
