Explanation

The contract 0xbca3…da4d is the vETH2 Minter of SharedStake, a liquid staking pool. Validators whose first deposit it relayed, and those withdrawing to the address they later set, are attributed to SharedStake vETH2 as allocator. It relayed the deposits of 500 validators, 16,000 ETH, between February and April 2021; 40 remained active in October 2026, with 1,280 ETH.

Identification. SharedStake's list of its contracts names this contract as its Minter, responsible for minting and burning vETH2 and for staking ether to the beacon chain, and its documentation explains that the Minter is paused while SharedStake deploys validators to the deposit contract. Its validator configuration sets the graffiti "SharedStake.org Prysm Validators".

On chain. All 500 deposits were sent through the Minter by 0xa1fe…409e (sharedstake.eth), with BLS credentials. 490 of the validators later changed to that address as their withdrawal address; the other ten still have BLS credentials. The address is the fee recipient of nearly all their blocks, and most blocks carry SharedStake's graffiti. SharedStake's later deployment script names the same address as its node operator.

The withdrawal address is an account SharedStake's developer also uses for deployments, so the withdrawals may go to SharedStake's operator rather than to vETH2 holders; the validators were funded by the pool either way. The decision would change with a SharedStake statement contradicting it, or evidence that validators deposited through the Minter were not SharedStake's.

What would change it

A SharedStake statement contradicting it, or evidence that validators deposited through the Minter were not SharedStake's.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
