Explanation

The contract 0x4f4b…dcf1 is the Permissionless Node Registry of Stader's liquid staking token ETHx. Validators whose first deposit it sent, and those withdrawing to the vaults those deposits name, are attributed to Stader ETHx as allocator.

Identification. Stader's ETHx repository lists its mainnet contracts, naming this one as its Permissionless Node Registry. Every ETHx validator's first deposit, of 1 ETH, is sent by one of two contracts on that list: the Stake Pool Manager (decision 0151) or the Permissionless Node Registry (decision 0152). Each deposit names the validator's own withdrawal vault.

On chain. 3,383 validators' first deposits came through this contract between June 2023 and March 2026, each to a different withdrawal address, Stader's per-validator vaults. Together the two contracts deposited 8,457 validators from June 2023; about 2,270 were active in October 2026, with some 73k ETH.

Why Stader is the allocator. ETHx assigns stakers' ETH to its node operators by its own rules, so Stader chooses the operators. Some operators sign their own names in graffiti; before this decision 65 validators had been attributed to those operators on that basis, and this claim now takes precedence. The decision would change with a Stader statement contradicting it, or evidence that the contract deposited for others.

What would change it

A Stader statement contradicting it, or evidence the contract deposited for others.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
