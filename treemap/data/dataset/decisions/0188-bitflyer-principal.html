Grounds

Explanation

bitFlyer, the Japanese exchange, is the principal of the validators withdrawing to the multisig wallet 0x51b4…b913: the ETH is bitFlyer's, staked for its customers, and bitFlyer hired the providers that run it. In October 2026 about 8,080 of these validators were active, with some 0.26M ETH.

The deposits. Tracing the multisig's deposit transactions shows that it called providers' batch deposit contracts rather than the deposit contract itself: Kiln's (decision 0034) for 3,838 validators between May and August 2025, all since exited, and Blockdaemon's (decision 0041) for 8,084 between May 2025 and February 2026. Those providers are the validators' allocators; this decision names the client that hired them.

The link to bitFlyer. Decision 0156 sets out why the multisig is taken to be bitFlyer's: its staking began alongside the launch of bitFlyer's ETH staking service in August 2025, and its holdings are consistent with the ETH reported in bitFlyer's financial statements for 2025. The link is strongly suggestive but not conclusive.

The decision would change with an address list or statement from bitFlyer that excludes this multisig, or evidence that it stakes for someone other than bitFlyer.

What would change it

An address list or statement from bitFlyer that excludes this multisig, or evidence that it stakes for someone other than bitFlyer.
