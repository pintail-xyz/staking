Explanation

The account 0x607e…31b7 made the deposits for the liquid staking of StakeHound, and the validators it deposited are attributed to StakeHound as allocator. It deposited 2,045 validators between December 2020 and May 2023, making up 1.2% of all stake at their peak in 2021. 872 withdraw to 0xf272…9394 and exited in 2023; 1,172, holding 37,504 ETH, are still active.

The graffiti. Every one of the 1,863 validators it deposited that has proposed a block signed "StakeHound ⚡️Allnodes" on its blocks, apart from some blocks carrying client version strings only. The validators name StakeHound, and Allnodes, its node provider, throughout.

The lost key. The 1,172 validators still active have kept their original BLS withdrawal credentials and have no execution withdrawal address. Their 37,504 ETH of principal is consistent with the 38,178 ETH that StakeHound said in 2021 had been locked after the shares of its withdrawal key were lost, as reported at the time; StakeHound sued its custody provider, which disputed responsibility, and ended its staking service in July 2021. Who runs those validators now is not known.

Limits. Graffiti is chosen by whoever runs a validator, and anyone could type a name; validators signing StakeHound's name on every block are taken to be StakeHound's. No operator is recorded. Explorer labels and community label sets are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that the account deposited for others.

What would change it

Evidence the account deposited for others, or that the tag is wrong.
