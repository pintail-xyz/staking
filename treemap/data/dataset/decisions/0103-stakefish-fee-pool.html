This contract, the fee recipient of blocks signed only with stakefish's graffiti and with the interface of stakefish's documented fee pool, is stakefish's fee pool.

Anchor · decided 2026-10-17

This contract is stakefish's fee pool; otherwise unattributed validators paying their fees to it are run and allocated by stakefish.

Explanation

The contract 0xffee…143b is stakefish's fee pool: the validators stakefish runs for its partners' customers pay their transaction fees and MEV to it, and it shares them out among those validators. Validators otherwise unattributed whose blocks paid it are attributed to stakefish as allocator for as long as they did. In October 2026 about 670 validators relied on this decision, with some 78k ETH, many of them compounding.

Identification. stakefish's documentation describes its fee pool, a smoothing pool it deploys and runs for the validators it operates (claiming rewards), though without giving its address. This contract, a proxy created in September 2022, has the interface of that pool. Every one of the 14,637 blocks naming it as their own fee recipient is signed with stakefish's graffiti ("Stake Earn Relax 🐠 stakefish") or left blank, and of the 13,509 validators that have paid it, 11,222 are already stakefish's by their withdrawal addresses.

As for stakefish's deposit contract (decision 0038), a fee recipient used only by validators signing stakefish's name is taken to be stakefish's. A fee pool claim yields to every other allocator claim. An explorer label is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with a stakefish statement contradicting it, or evidence that the pool takes validators stakefish does not run.

What would change it

A stakefish statement contradicting it, or evidence the pool takes validators stakefish does not run.
