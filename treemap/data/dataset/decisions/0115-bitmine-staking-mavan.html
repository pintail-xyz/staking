Explanation

Validators withdrawing to these 81 addresses are also BitMine Immersion Technologies' treasury staking, beyond the 111 addresses of decision 0087. In October 2026 they held about 1,150 active validators with some 2.0M ETH; together with 0087 this puts BitMine's staking at about 5.1M ETH, against the 5,067,309 ETH it reported staked.

The addresses. Each deposits compounding (0x02) validators of about 1,800 to 1,900 ETH to itself, as BitMine's other wallets do. Most of their stake went through Coinbase's batch deposit contract, the rest through two batch contracts that BitMine's other wallets also used, or directly. 75 of the 79 addresses with active validators have validators on one fleet, recognisable by a Nethermind build that appears on no other validators: BitMine's validators were the first to run it, on 12 May 2026, and the fleet has changed versions together since, alternating with a Geth and Prysm build also seen only on BitMine's validators, and moving to Reth from 13 October 2026. About 1.19M ETH of these addresses' stake is on that fleet, about 0.56M ETH on validators running Nimbus with its default graffiti, and the rest on smaller setups. BitMine owns the staking platform MAVAN, launched in March 2026 on the staking provider Pier Two, which its subsidiary MAVAN Holdings bought that month (quarterly report). The fleets are not shown to be MAVAN's: MAVAN's validators in Lido run widely used release builds, not this one.

Common control and the match with BitMine's figures. 74 of the 81 took part in the control transfers of 31 August 2026 described under decision 0087, paying the same two new accounts in the same 73 minutes. BitMine publishes its staked ETH every week. Across all 41 weekly releases from 4 January 2026 to 11 October 2026, the ETH deposited by these addresses and by those of 0087 matches the reported figure to within 0.033M ETH, and every increase falls in the same week as the reported one.

The seven addresses that did not take part in the control transfers hold 68,988 ETH. They are linked to one another by their funding but not to the others, and they rest on running on the same fleets, depositing the same way, and on the weekly match, which they are needed for: without them the staked total falls further below BitMine's figure. BitMine has said MAVAN would also serve outside clients, so these seven could belong to another client of the same operators.

Explorer labels on the brokers that funded many of these wallets are recorded as leads. The decision would change if BitMine's reported staked ETH diverged from these deposits, or with evidence that an address belongs to another holder.

What would change it

BitMine's reported staked ETH diverging from these deposits, or evidence an address belongs to another holder.
