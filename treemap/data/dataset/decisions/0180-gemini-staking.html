Explanation

Validators withdrawing to these nine addresses are Gemini's ETH staking, with Gemini as allocator. In October 2026 about 3,260 of them were active, with some 0.22M ETH; four of the addresses held validators from 2021 to 2025 that have all exited.

The link through Gemini's own token. Gemini issues the GUSD stablecoin, whose contract Gemini's GUSD page gives; Gemini's annual report on Form 10-K calls GUSD a stablecoin issued by Gemini. Reading GUSD's contracts on chain gives the account allowed to mint it, 0xd244…6853, and shows another, 0x4c2f…5346, acting as the contracts' custodian, an action only the custodian can take. Separately, a tracing expert's declaration filed in a 2022 court case lists 0xd244…6853 as a Gemini address.

Control and funding. 0x4c2f…5346 is the account that operates six of the withdrawal addresses, which are wallet contracts built from two shared implementations; it also made the deposits for four of them, and 0xd244…6853 has paid its gas since 2017. Every depositor of the other addresses was funded by 0xd244…6853 or by 0xf517…, a high-volume hot wallet that 0xd244…6853 funds. The two largest groups, deposited from 2023 to 2026, went through Staked's batch deposit contract (Staked's repository); the newer compounding validators, from November 2025, were deposited directly.

What Gemini says. Its annual report says it provides ETH staking using staking validators it controls, and its quarterly report for mid-2026 describes building out in-house validator capabilities, matching the new compounding fleets from November 2025. Staked may have run the earlier validators, but no operator is recorded.

Other accounts that Gemini's wallets funded look like customers' withdrawals, spread across many providers, and are not attributed. Explorer labels on Gemini's wallets are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that 0xd244…6853 or 0x4c2f…5346 is not Gemini's, or that this stake is a single client's held outside Gemini's staking.

What would change it

Evidence that 0xd244 or 0x4c2f is not Gemini's, or that this stake is a single client's held outside Gemini's staking.
