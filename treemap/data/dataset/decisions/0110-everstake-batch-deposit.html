Explanation

The contract 0x4ff4…7e6d is Everstake's batch deposit contract: validators first deposited through it are attributed to Everstake as allocator and operator, while each client keeps its own withdrawal address. 66 validators were deposited through it between September 2025 and January 2026, from ten sending accounts, each withdrawing to itself; 65 were active in October 2026 with about 106k ETH, almost all of it in consolidated compounding validators.

Identification. Everstake's blog announces the audit of its ETH2 batch deposit contract and links the auditor's report, which gives this contract's mainnet address. On chain, it was created by the same account that created Everstake's pooled-staking contract (decision 0003). Its validators sign their blocks "Everstake / Pro", and all pay the same fee recipient, a Safe multisig shared with the validators of Everstake's second batch contract.

Why Everstake is the allocator. A client depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider, who generates the keys and runs the validators; the client keeps the withdrawal address. This is the rule applied to all providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005). The contract is open to anyone, so a deposit through it does not by itself prove that Everstake runs the validator; the graffiti corroborates it.

The decision would change with an Everstake statement contradicting it, or evidence that some depositors use the contract with keys Everstake does not run.

What would change it
