Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0xedc6…87c9 or 0xfc57…d030 are the staking of Bitvavo, the Dutch exchange, with Bitvavo as allocator. In October 2026 they held 522 active validators with about 64k ETH: twenty compounding validators at the first address and 502 at the second.

The anchor: Bitvavo's hot wallet. In a discussion on the ENS governance forum in December 2022, a customer who had withdrawn ENS tokens from Bitvavo to the wrong address relayed Bitvavo's answer: 0x95b5…cc8a is its hot wallet, and a refund sent there would be credited. The refund was sent there, and the customer confirmed that the tokens were back. On chain, the customer's withdrawal came from that wallet, and the customer's Bitvavo deposit address swept tokens into it: the wallet both pays Bitvavo's withdrawals and collects its deposits.

From the hot wallet to the staking. 0xedc6…87c9 refills that hot wallet and a second one, 0x079a…dc63, which pays the first one's gas: a cold wallet refilling the exchange's hot wallets. 0xedc6…87c9 deposited validators itself, and funded 0xfc57…d030, which deposited its own validators, partly through Kiln's and Coinbase's batch deposit contracts and partly directly. Another wallet in the same circle of transfers is 0xfc57…d030's main funder.

What Bitvavo says. Bitvavo says it stakes customers' assets itself. Its proof of reserves at 30 June 2026 counts 267,278 ETH, including staked ETH under keys Bitvavo controls. One address's validators sign P2P.org's graffiti, as their operator; no operator is recorded here.

Limits. Bitvavo's statement reaches this record through its customer, and the staking addresses are tied to the hot wallet by ETH flows rather than by a Bitvavo statement. Community trackers and explorer labels listing the wallets as Bitvavo's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that either address holds a client's ETH rather than Bitvavo's, or a Bitvavo statement that 0x95b5…cc8a is not its wallet.

What would change it

Evidence that 0xedc6 or 0xfc57 hold a client's ETH rather than Bitvavo's, or a Bitvavo statement that 0x95b5 is not its wallet.
