Grounds

  • First partyKraken announced on 21 December 2021 that it had acquired Staked
  • First partyStaked's website now says Staked has moved to Kraken Staking and redirects to Kraken's institutional staking page

Explanation

Kraken announced on 21 December 2021 that it had acquired Staked, a non-custodial staking provider. Staked's website now says that Staked has moved to Kraken Staking. Staked is shown as owned by Kraken from the date of the announcement: the stake Staked allocates and the validators it runs (among them those of Kraken's own staking) count towards Kraken from that date. The boxes of the treemap do not move: who allocates their stake is unchanged.

What would change it

A later sale of Staked, or a Kraken statement that the acquisition did not complete.
