Explanation

The account 0x4489…7f97 makes the beacon deposits for Upbit's ETH staking, and validators withdrawing to its withdrawal address, 0x7e2a…2d05, are attributed to Upbit as allocator. 37,657 validators have withdrawn to that address; about 20,100 were active in October 2026 with some 0.64M ETH. Upbit runs its own validators, but no operator claim is made.

What Upbit says. Dunamu, Upbit's operator, states in its half-year report for 2026 that Upbit Staking runs its own validators and nodes, keeps staked assets in cold wallets, and began staking with Ethereum in 2022. It publishes no Ethereum addresses. In June 2026 Dunamu announced that Upbit was the largest of Polygon's 102 validators, with about 413 million POL. On Polygon's staking contract at that date exactly 102 validators were active and the largest held 413.25 million POL, so that validator is Upbit's; its owner and signer accounts are on Ethereum.

The deposits. Every validator withdrawing to 0x7e2a…2d05 was first deposited by one of 13 accounts used in sequence: twelve from January 2022 to April 2023 (with BLS credentials, later changed to 0x7e2a…2d05), then 0x4489…7f97 from April 2023. Each account began where the previous one stopped, and each received a 0-ETH transaction from the same account just before it started depositing: one operator setting up each new depositor.

The link to Upbit. The 13 accounts received 1,215,587 ETH. 96% came from a closed network of wallets which deal almost only with one another, an operator's internal wallet system rather than a public venue; most of the rest is restaked rewards. Every gas payment ever made to the signer of Upbit's Polygon validator (21 payments) came from wallets in that same network, and its owner account was funded by another. Withdrawn stake returns to the network.

Limits. The link from Dunamu's statements to these addresses runs through the Polygon validator and the shared wallets; Dunamu names no Ethereum address. Since the funding network is closed and covers every deposit since 2022, a customer staking independently would have to share Upbit's internal wallets. Explorer labels and community label sets naming the accounts are recorded as leads. The decision would change with an Upbit statement contradicting it, or evidence that the funding wallets or the withdrawal address belong to someone other than Upbit.

What would change it

An Upbit statement contradicting it, or evidence that the funding wallets or the withdrawal address belong to someone other than Upbit.
