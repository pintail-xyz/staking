Explanation

The address 0x4675…a263 is Coinbase's fee recipient: the validators Coinbase runs pay their transaction fees and MEV to it. Validators otherwise unattributed whose blocks paid it are attributed to Coinbase as allocator for as long as they did. In October 2026 about 1,030 validators relied on this decision, with some 33k ETH.

Who pays it. Of the 127,975 validators whose blocks have paid it, 126,944 are already Coinbase's by other evidence (Coinbase's retail staking, decisions 0016, 0017 and 0020), so it is the fee recipient of Coinbase's own validators rather than a client's. The remaining 1,031 paid it in 99.6% of their blocks, including through other block builders' payments to it as the fee recipient they had registered, so they chose it. Which Coinbase service these belong to, for example Prime or Cloud clients, is not known. The address never pays proposers, so it is not a block builder.

Where its ETH goes. From mid-2024 to October 2026 it made over 82,000 payments, totalling 23,351 ETH. 95.5% went to wallets established as Coinbase's from chain data alone: the wallet that funds Coinbase's retail staking deposits, the wallet that receives all newly minted cbETH, and the payout wallets of Coinbase's staking sweeps. Most of the rest went to one wallet not identified.

A fee recipient claim yields to every other allocator claim. An explorer label on the address is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with evidence that clients of other providers also use this address as their fee recipient.

What would change it

Evidence that clients of other providers also use this address as their fee recipient.
