Lido's operator group 4.14 ("Stakin by The Tie") and the operators it lists are run by Stakin.

Operator or ownerOperated by Stakin from 2022-01-14, for Stakin by The Tie, Stakin by The Tie, Stakin by The Tie - PTO · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's operator group 4.14 ("Stakin by The Tie") and the operators it lists are run by Stakin.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.14, "Stakin by The Tie", from 2 August 2026
  • On chainIt lists Lido operators 1/14, 4/18
  • On chainThe first of them, 1/14, was registered on 14 January 2022

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.14, "Stakin by The Tie", from 2 August 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/14 ("Stakin by The Tie"), 4/18 ("Stakin by The Tie - PTO"). The group's name is matched to Stakin, and its operators are recorded as run by Stakin from the first one's registration on 14 January 2022.

What would change it

A Lido or Stakin statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
