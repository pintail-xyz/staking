Explanation

The contract 0xb60b…2f31 is taken to be a later version of Everstake's batch deposit contract: validators first deposited through it are attributed to Everstake as allocator and operator, while the client keeps its own withdrawal address. 27 compounding validators, about 50k ETH, were deposited through it on 15 April 2026 by one account withdrawing to itself; all were active in October 2026.

Identification. No published source names this contract. It was created by the same account that created Everstake's audited batch deposit contract (decision 0110) and Everstake's pooled-staking contract (decision 0003). Its verified source carries the same contract name as the audited contract, with different code, as a later version would. Its validators sign their blocks "Everstake / Pro" and pay the same fee recipient, a Safe multisig, as the audited contract's validators do.

It is kept as its own decision because its support is weaker than the audited contract's. A deployment account could in principle deploy contracts for others. The decision would change with evidence that the creating account deploys contracts for others, or an Everstake statement contradicting it.
