Grounds

Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x7317…89e9 are the staking of HashKey Exchange, the Hong Kong exchange, with HashKey Exchange as allocator. 140 compounding validators were deposited between March and October 2026; 88 were active in October 2026 with about 7,000 ETH.

The anchor. In a change to DefiLlama's adapter for HashKey Exchange, merged in March 2025, a HashKey employee, whose public profile shows a hashkey.com email address, added 0xee1b…7ad9 as HashKey Exchange's second ETH cold wallet, citing the transfer of 65,340 ETH to it from the first.

On chain. That cold wallet sent all 140 deposits of the validators withdrawing to 0x7317…89e9, and no other deposits came from it, from the first cold wallet or from the withdrawal address. The withdrawal address is the fee recipient of all their blocks, has swept 4,732 ETH of rewards back to the cold wallet, and has itself sent 35 exit and partial-withdrawal requests: whoever holds it runs the stake and returns its rewards to HashKey's cold wallet.

The operator. The blocks carry only client tags, so no operator is recorded. HashKey Cloud, HashKey Group's staking business, says it runs staking for an Ethereum ETF with HashKey Exchange as custodian; some of this stake may be the ETF's, held by HashKey Exchange, which chose how to stake it either way. The decision would change with evidence that the withdrawal address holds a client's ETH under another allocator, or a HashKey statement that the cold wallet is not its own.

What would change it

Evidence that 0x7317 holds a client's ETH under another allocator, or a HashKey statement that 0xee1b is not its wallet.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
