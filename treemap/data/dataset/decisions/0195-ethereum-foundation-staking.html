Explanation

Validators withdrawing to the Safe multisig 0xc061…0b6d are the Ethereum Foundation's treasury staking, with the Foundation as allocator. There are 34 compounding validators, holding 69,632 ETH in October 2026, all previously unattributed. The Foundation stakes its own treasury and says it runs the validators itself; operators are left to graffiti.

What the Foundation published. The Foundation's announcement of 24 February 2026 describes staking about 70,000 ETH with compounding (0x02) credentials across roughly 35 keys, and links the first of these validators. That validator withdraws to this Safe, and received 2,016 ETH on the day of the announcement. The Safe now has 34 validators holding 69,632 ETH.

On chain. The first validators' initial deposits were sent through other Safes, one of which was created by an owner of this Safe; the Safe itself then topped each validator up to about 2,048 ETH between 30 March and 3 April 2026. The Safe has seven owners and a threshold of four. It also received 160,000 ETH in one transfer from the Foundation's long-standing wallet, which is widely known but not relied on here.

Explorer labels naming the Safes as the Foundation's are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Foundation statement naming other validators, or evidence that the Safe is not the Foundation's.

What would change it

An Ethereum Foundation statement naming other validators, or evidence that the Safe is not the EF's.
