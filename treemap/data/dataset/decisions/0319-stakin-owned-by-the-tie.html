Stakin is owned by The Tie from 12 January 2026.

Grounds

  • First partyStakin announced on 12 January 2026 that it had been acquired by The Tie
  • On chainLido's registry renamed its operator 1/14 "Stakin by The Tie" on 17 April 2026

Explanation

Stakin announced on 12 January 2026 that it had been acquired by The Tie, and continues to run its Lido operators, which the registry renamed "Stakin by The Tie" on 17 April 2026. Stakin is shown as owned by The Tie from the announcement; who runs and allocates its operators' stake is unchanged.

What would change it

A later sale of Stakin, or a statement that the acquisition did not complete.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
