0326-kraken-staking

Name Kraken Staking (formerly Staked) · decided 2026-11-04

Explanation

Staked, owned by Kraken since 21 December 2021 (decision 0325), now operates as Kraken Staking: its website says that Staked has moved to Kraken Staking and sends its users to Kraken's staking dashboard. No dated announcement of the move was found. The Internet Archive's captures of the site date it: on 30 March 2026 it was still Staked's own site, and on 18 April 2026 it carried the notice, so Staked is shown under its former name until 18 April 2026, the first date the move is recorded. The name keeps "formerly Staked" because Kraken's own exchange staking is a separate box named "Kraken staking".
