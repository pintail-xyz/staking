Explanation

The account 0xed20…8ab0 makes deposits for Kraken's staking, and validators withdrawing to its addresses are attributed to Kraken's staking as allocator. It deposited 8,173 validators between April 2025 and February 2026, through the same batch deposit contract as Kraken's main deposit account (decision 0007); in October 2026 about 880 of its validators, mostly compounding, were active with some 0.23M ETH.

The link to Kraken. On 21 March 2025 the address 0x210b…2a1b, one of Kraken's withdrawal addresses under decision 0007, gave this account its first 0.1 ETH of gas. Within the same 40 minutes it gave the first gas to Kraken's EigenLayer restaking account (decision 0010) and to another deposit account (decision 0009). One account paying first gas to all three in the same window is taken to hold them all. The same address then sent this account 33 ETH just before its first deposit, and 32,906 ETH before its first large batch.

The pattern. Like Kraken's other accounts in 2025, it deposited most validators with its own address as withdrawal address. It moved its stake by consolidation in the same weeks of March and June 2026 as Kraken did, partly into an address that carries the same contract code as one of Kraken's own consolidation targets.

Limits. The batch contract it used has no caller check, so using it does not by itself show that Kraken sent a deposit; the link rests on the funding. The identification is also only as strong as decision 0010, which rests on Kraken's announced restaking and on its EigenLayer operator's self-published metadata. Whether Kraken holds this stake for itself or for a client, Kraken chose who runs the validators, which makes it the allocator. Explorer labels are recorded as leads. The decision would change with a Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence that the account deposits keys a client chose.

What would change it

A Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence it deposits keys chosen by a client.
