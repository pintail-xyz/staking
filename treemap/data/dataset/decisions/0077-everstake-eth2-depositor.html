Explanation

The account 0xf79c…00b9 made Everstake's first staking deposits, and validators withdrawing to the address its validators moved to, the multisig wallet 0x2bf9…4087, are attributed to Everstake as allocator. 12,567 validators have withdrawn to that wallet; about 9,400 were active in October 2026 with some 0.30M ETH.

The chain of control. The account deposited 200 validators in November 2020, all with BLS credentials. By June 2023 all 200 had been changed to 0x2bf9…4087, a 2-of-4 Safe multisig created that month; only the holder of their keys could make the change. Two of the Safe's owners then deposited 12,345 more validators to it in 2024, through a batch contract, and a third owner created the Safe.

The graffiti. Of the 12,499 validators withdrawing to the Safe that have proposed a block, 12,486 have signed graffiti beginning "Everstake / Pro"; twelve only left blocks blank and one signed only a client version string. A fleet of this size signing Everstake's name on nearly every block is taken to be Everstake's.

Limits. Graffiti is chosen by whoever runs a validator, and anyone could type a name; the identification rests on that, and on the chain of control from the first deposits. It also suggests Everstake runs the validators, though no operator is recorded. Whether the ETH is Everstake's own or its clients' is not established. Explorer labels and community label sets naming the accounts are recorded as leads. The decision would change with evidence that the Safe belongs to a client of Everstake rather than to Everstake.

What would change it

Evidence that address belongs to an Everstake client rather than Everstake.
