Explanation

The account 0xbdd7…32a8 makes the beacon deposits for Binance's ETH staking. Validators withdrawing to an address its deposits used are attributed to Binance's staking as allocator. In practice there is one such address, 0x8e60…8224: 123,016 validators have withdrawn to it, of which 76,900 were active in October 2026 with about 3.49M ETH. Binance decides who runs this stake; whether Binance runs the validators itself is not established, so no operator is recorded.

The deposit pattern. Between October 2021 and January 2026 the account sent 99,749 deposits. Every one with execution-layer credentials (90,576) names 0x8e60…8224; the other 9,173 used BLS credentials. All 32,319 validators in the group with BLS credentials changed to 0x8e60…8224 in April 2023, immediately after withdrawals were enabled, which shows a single holder of all their keys. Of the group's 26 deposit senders, 22, covering 121,893 validators including 0xbdd7…32a8, received their first ETH from 0xf17a…4a18, an account whose 524 transactions consist of deposits, the funding of these senders, and transfers to Binance's own wallets.

The link to Binance runs through wBETH, Binance's wrapped staked ETH, whose Ethereum contract BNB Chain's announcement of the token names. wBETH's verified source sends staked ETH to the address returned by ethReceiver(), which on chain is 0x26ad…6b86, set at the contract's initialisation and unchanged since. The withdrawal address 0x8e60…8224 has made 46 transactions in all: a 0.0001 ETH test transfer and 45 transfers to 0x26ad…6b86 totalling 1,739,061 ETH, the withdrawn stake. The deposit account and its funder have also paid 0x26ad…6b86 directly. wBETH's supply on Ethereum, about 3.52M ETH at its exchange rate, is close to the stake of these validators, though wBETH also exists on BNB Chain and need not correspond one to one.

Binance's proof-of-reserves list of 1 October 2026 does not name this account or 0x8e60…8224, but one of its listed wallets, and an account funded only by listed wallets, made deposits naming 0x8e60…8224 in 2026, which decision 0323 records. Explorer labels and community label sets naming the accounts as Binance's are recorded as leads, not relied on. The decision would change with a Binance statement or proof-of-reserves list contradicting it, or evidence that 0x8e60…8224 holds stake for a client rather than for Binance's staking product.

What would change it

A Binance statement or proof-of-reserves list contradicting it, or evidence the address holds stake for a client.
