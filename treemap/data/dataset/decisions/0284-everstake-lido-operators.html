Lido's operator group 4.25 ("Everstake") and the operators it lists are run by Everstake.

Operator or ownerOperated by Everstake from 2021-04-14, for Everstake, Everstake, Everstake PTO, Everstake DO · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's operator group 4.25 ("Everstake") and the operators it lists are run by Everstake.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.25, "Everstake", from 3 August 2026
  • On chainIt lists Lido operators 1/7, 4/15, 4/33
  • On chainThe first of them, 1/7, was registered on 14 April 2021

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.25, "Everstake", from 3 August 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/7 ("Everstake"), 4/15 ("Everstake PTO"), 4/33 ("Everstake DO"). The group's name is matched to Everstake, and its operators are recorded as run by Everstake from the first one's registration on 14 April 2021.

What would change it

A Lido or Everstake statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
