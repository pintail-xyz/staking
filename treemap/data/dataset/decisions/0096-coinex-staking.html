Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0xafed…4399 or 0x5480…fcb2, or deposited by 0x38f6…6336, were the staking of CoinEx, the exchange, with CoinEx as allocator. 1,027 validators withdrew to the first address from April 2024, and 482 were deposited by the third account in June and July 2025, almost all to the second; all have since exited. The stake was CoinEx's customers'.

CoinEx's proof of reserves. For each of its proof-of-reserves snapshots CoinEx publishes a file of its wallet addresses, each with a signature of the message "I am a CoinEx address" (for example the snapshots of 13 November 2024 and 4 June 2025). The signature for 0x38f6…6336 recovers that address. 0xafed…4399 is a multisig wallet with fifteen owners; its listed signer is one of them and the signature recovers it. 0x5480…fcb2 is signed for by another owner of the same multisig.

The validators themselves. The same files list CoinEx's staked validators by public key, with these withdrawal addresses: 529 at the first address in January 2025, falling to 311 by October 2025, then 311 and later 24 at the second from May 2026. CoinEx notes that its wallet assets include on-chain funds managed under staking.

No operator is recorded. A community-compiled reserve list is recorded as a lead. The decision would change if CoinEx's own disclosures dropped the addresses, or with evidence that CoinEx holds them for a client.

What would change it

CoinEx's own disclosures dropping the addresses, or evidence it holds them for a client.
