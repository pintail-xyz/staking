Explanation

The contract 0x1e68…0270 is Kiln's dedicated-staking contract, through which users of the Ledger Live wallet staked ETH. Validators first deposited through it are attributed to Kiln as allocator and operator, while each staker kept their own withdrawal address. 25,909 validators were deposited through it between November 2022 and September 2025, from 6,333 sending accounts, each validator with its own withdrawal address; they held as much as 460k ETH. All had exited by November 2025, after the Ledger Live staking application was retired.

Identification. Kiln's documentation names this contract as the one behind dedicated staking from Ledger Live.

Why Kiln is the allocator. A staker depositing through a provider's contract has chosen that provider, who generates the keys and runs the validators; the staker keeps the withdrawal address. This is the rule applied to all providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005), and it holds where the staker reaches the provider through a wallet integration such as Ledger Live.

The decision would change with a Kiln statement contradicting it, or evidence that some depositors used the contract with keys Kiln did not run.
