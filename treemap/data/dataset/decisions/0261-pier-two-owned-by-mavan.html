MAVAN owns Pier Two from 24 March 2026.

Operator or ownerOwned by MAVAN from 2026-03-24, for Pier Two · decided 2026-10-28

Pier Two is owned by MAVAN Holdings from 24 March 2026.

Grounds

  • First partyBitMine's 8-K of 24 March 2026 reports its subsidiary Standard Validator LLC buying all shares of Pier Two Holdings
  • First partyStandard Validator LLC is now named MAVAN Holdings

Explanation

BitMine Immersion Technologies reported on 24 March 2026 that Standard Validator LLC, its majority-owned subsidiary, was the buyer of all outstanding shares of Pier Two Holdings Pty Ltd. Standard Validator LLC is now named MAVAN Holdings (BitMine's quarterly report to 31 May 2026). Pier Two is shown as owned by MAVAN from that date.

What would change it

A later sale of Pier Two.
