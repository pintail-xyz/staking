Explanation

These 112 validators are the 33% share of the iShares Staked Ethereum Trust's (ETHB's) custody deposits and Figment's part of its seed deposits (13 validators), and are attributed to Figment as allocator. In November 2026, 106 of them were active, holding 117,307 ETH; 6 have exited.

ETHB's providers. BlackRock's trust listed on 12 March 2026. Its prospectus says that the sponsor chooses the staking providers and allocates ether among them, and that "the Approved Validators are Figment Inc., Galaxy Blockchain Infrastructure LLC and Attestant Limited". The ether is held by Coinbase Custody, and Galaxy describes itself as one of three firms selected.

The deposits. From 13 March 2026, the day after listing, until 15 September 2026, 61 deposit transactions through Coinbase's batch deposit contract, each sent by the custody address its validators withdraw to, split their ether in the same proportions: 40% to one set of validators, 33% to a second and 27% to a third. No custody deposit before the listing has this pattern. The three shares behave as three different operators. The 40% share signs Galaxy's name in its graffiti. The 33% share signs a bare Geth and Lighthouse client string, as Figment's own validators do, and buys almost all its blocks from relays. The 27% share builds nine in ten of its blocks locally, with blank or Besu extra data, and moves through the same client builds, on the same days, as the Lido validators Attestant runs. This decision covers the 33% share; Galaxy's (40%) and Attestant's (27%) shares are covered by their own decisions.

The seed deposits. On 7 March 2026, before listing, seven custody deposits through the same contract split 41,132 ETH in two: 53% to validators that sign Galaxy's name, 47% to validators that sign Figment's bare client string and buy almost all their blocks from relays. One of these custody addresses made one of the 40/33/27 deposits on 16 March, so the seed lots are the trust's too.

The totals. ETHB's holdings file gave 337,754 ETH staked on 2 November 2026; the validators of the seed and 40/33/27 deposits held about 337,300 ETH two days later. Its quarterly report gave 250,585 ETH staked on 30 June 2026, when 249,943 ETH had been deposited. The trust is recorded as these validators' principal (decision 0334). The decision would change if ETHB or Figment said this share is run by another firm, or if these validators' behaviour diverged from Figment's.

What would change it

ETHB or Figment saying this share is run by another firm, these deposits proving not to be ETHB's and Figment not to run them, or these validators' behaviour diverging from Figment's.
