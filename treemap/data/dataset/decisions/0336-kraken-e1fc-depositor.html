Explanation

The account 0xe1fc…8267 makes deposits for Kraken's staking. From February 2024 it deposited 1,204 validators through Figment's depositor contract (0xf2be…dab1), each withdrawing to the account itself, which is also its validators' fee recipient.

The link to Kraken. Its first ETH, in February 2024, came from 0x39ed…82e5, Kraken's deposit account under decision 0335, which sent it 640 ETH in all before its first deposits. Both accounts deposit through the same contract, to themselves, and both exchanged ETH with the same unidentified counterparty 0x4063…c84d.

Who runs the validators. From 19 March 2025, Kraken Staking (formerly Staked), which Kraken owns (decision 0325), registered 1,144 of the account's validators on the SSV network from its own account, on clusters made only of its own operators, the same day it began doing so for 0x39ed's. Kraken is their allocator from that registration; before it, Figment is the allocator and Kraken the principal (decision 0337). The other 60 validators stay Figment's, with Kraken as principal.

Limits. The identification is only as strong as decision 0335. The decision would change with a Kraken statement contradicting it, or evidence that the account deposits on a client's instruction.

What would change it

A Kraken statement contradicting it, decision 0335 being reverted, or evidence that the account deposits for a client who chose the operators.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
