Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0xcc31…df43 and 0xdd6a…b30a are BitMine Immersion Technologies' treasury staking, with BitMine as allocator. In October 2026 the two addresses held 36 compounding validators with about 47k ETH, deposited from October 2025.

Common control. Both addresses took part in the control transfers of 31 August 2026 described under decision 0087, sending 0.001 ETH to the same two new accounts within the same 73 minutes as all 111 of BitMine's wallets in that decision.

The same way of staking. Each deposits compounding validators to itself, through the same two batch contracts BitMine's other wallets used. The first address's validators run on the same SSV operators as part of decision 0087's validators, registered by an owner account funded by the same account as those of 0087. The second's validators run on two of the fleets that also run validators of decisions 0087 and 0115: Nimbus with its default graffiti, and the Nethermind build seen only on BitMine's validators.

The match with BitMine's figures. BitMine's first weekly figure, 659,219 ETH staked as of 4 January 2026, is 6% above the deposits of decisions 0087 and 0115 by then; with these two addresses the deposits are 1% above it. Their figures as of 30 August 2026 are also part of the match described under decision 0087 (release).

Limits. As for decision 0087, the control transfers show one holder, or parties acting together, and the identification with BitMine rests on the match with its reported figures. These two addresses began staking about twelve weeks before BitMine's other wallets; that they fill the gap in its first weekly figure is the only evidence for the timing, and an outside client of the same providers would look the same. The decision would change with evidence that either address is held by someone other than BitMine, or with a provider identified for these validators.

What would change it

Evidence that either address is held by someone other than BitMine, or a provider identified for these validators.
