Explanation

The contract 0x0d6f…25bc is NodeDAO's restaking pool, whose token is rnETH (Restaking Node ETH). Validators whose first deposit it sent, and those withdrawing to the EigenLayer pod those deposits name, are attributed to NodeDAO rnETH as allocator. It sent 368 deposits between April 2024 and May 2025; 298 validators were active in October 2026 with about 9,500 ETH.

Identification. NodeDAO's repository lists its timelock controller but predates this contract. On chain, the pool's owner is that timelock, as is the owner of its pool token, "Restaking Node ETH" (rnETH). Every deposit was sent by the pool's validator manager, whose verified ENS name is nodedao.eth, and every deposit withdraws to one EigenLayer pod, whose owner is itself owned by the same timelock.

NodeDAO chooses the operators of its pool, so it is the allocator. No operator is recorded. The decision would change with a NodeDAO statement contradicting it, or evidence that the timelock or these contracts are not NodeDAO's.

What would change it

A NodeDAO statement contradicting it, or evidence that the timelock or these contracts are not NodeDAO's.
