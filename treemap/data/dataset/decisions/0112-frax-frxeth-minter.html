Explanation

The contract 0xbafa…1138 is the frxETH Minter of Frax Finance's liquid staking token frxETH. Validators withdrawing to the address it deposits to are attributed to Frax as allocator. 7,483 deposits were sent through it between October 2022 and June 2025, holding as much as 237k ETH; about 1,400 validators were active in October 2026 with some 45k ETH.

Identification. Frax's own code (a script in its frxETH repository) checks the validators that Frax's multisig wallet 0x8306…a81c queues, and requires each queued transaction to call this contract. Every deposit sent through the Minter withdraws to that multisig, and the multisig has received no deposits by any other route. The validators sign the graffiti "¤ Frax Finance ¤". Etherscan's address page attributes the contract to Frax Finance; that is recorded as third-party corroboration.

Why Frax is the allocator. Frax's multisig chooses the validator keys the Minter deposits with. No operator is recorded. No validator here has a conflicting allocator claim.

The decision would change with a Frax statement contradicting it, or deposits to the multisig by another route.

What would change it

A Frax statement contradicting it, or deposits to the same withdrawal address by another route.
