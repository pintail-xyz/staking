Explanation

The account 0x39ed…82e5 makes deposits for Kraken's staking. From June 2023 it deposited 4,538 validators through Figment's depositor contract (0xf2be…dab1), each withdrawing to the account itself, which is also its validators' fee recipient.

The link to Kraken. The account's first ETH, in June 2023, came from 0x267b…ffdc0. That wallet received 1.86M ETH in 35 transfers from 0x210b…2a1b, one of Kraken's withdrawal addresses under decision 0007. Over the following years 0x267b sent the account 24,333 ETH and the account sent 27,408 ETH back.

Who runs the validators. Figment's contract is open to anyone, but stake deposited through it is taken to be run by Figment (method provider.depositor_contract). From 19 March 2025, Kraken Staking (formerly Staked), which Kraken owns (decision 0325), registered 2,017 of the account's validators on the SSV network from its own account, on clusters made only of its own operators. The registering account splits the validator key into its shares, so Kraken Staking then held the keys. Under the allocator chain rule, Figment is the allocator while it ran a validator and Kraken its principal (decision 0337); once Kraken's own operator holds the keys, Kraken is the allocator. Some validators were registered on SSV within a day of their deposit, so Figment never ran those. The other 2,521 validators, most of them exited before March 2025, were never registered this way and stay Figment's, with Kraken as principal.

Limits. The link to Kraken rests on the funding through 0x267b, which no source names; the wallet receiving Kraken's withdrawn stake is taken to be Kraken's own. The account also exchanged ETH with 0x4063…c84d, which is not identified. The decision would change with a Kraken statement contradicting it, evidence that 0x267b is not Kraken's, or evidence that the account deposits on a client's instruction.

What would change it

A Kraken statement contradicting it, evidence that 0x267b…ffdc0 is not Kraken's, or evidence that the account deposits for a client who chose the operators.
