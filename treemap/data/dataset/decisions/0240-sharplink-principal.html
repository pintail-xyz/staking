Explanation

Sharplink, Inc. is the principal of the validators withdrawing to these 21 addresses: the ETH is Sharplink's, and Sharplink chose its providers. In October 2026 the addresses held about 9,400 active validators with some 301k ETH: about 179k in validators run by Figment, about 100k in validators deposited through Figment's batch contract whose blocks are all signed by Galaxy, and 22k in Coinbase's custody staking, in vaults of 3,200 ETH. Figment, Galaxy and Coinbase remain the allocators of these validators; this decision records whose stake it is.

The Ethereum Foundation's sale. Sharplink bought 10,000 ETH from the Ethereum Foundation in a transaction that closed on 10 July 2025. On 11 July 2025 the Foundation's address (decision 0195) sent exactly 10,000 ETH to 0x3536…4feb, a wallet of this group that otherwise received ETH only from 0x0b26…3e57 and passed it on to the group's LsETH wallet.

The wallets. One account, 0xcd9e…5f75, itself funded by a single account, 0x3356…6836, began passing ETH to the group on 31 May 2025, after Sharplink's private placement of 26 May; Sharplink's first treasury release reported 176,271 ETH on 13 June 2025, over 95% of it in staking and liquid staking. From there ETH was passed from wallet to wallet within the group, and each of the 21 addresses deposits validators through Figment's batch deposit contract or Coinbase's. Three of the addresses, 0x2560…5136 and the two it funded, received their ETH through single-use relay addresses from a trading account rather than from the group, but 0x2560…5136 also passed ETH to the group's wallet 0x24eb…b391.

The match with Sharplink's LsETH. Sharplink reports its holdings split into native ETH, LsETH and weETH. The group minted 256,474 ETH of LsETH between 30 May and 23 July 2025 and was paid redemptions of 35,627 ETH on 26 December 2025 and 16,740 ETH on 2 March 2026. With LsETH's growth from rewards, about 2.2% a year, that leaves about 224k ETH on 15 February and 208k on 31 March 2026. Sharplink reported 225,429 ETH on 15 February, and 209,361 on 31 March and 209,789 on 4 May 2026, with no redemption in between, as on chain. Both redemptions passed through 0x0323…91b1 into ether.fi, on 7 January and 6 March 2026; between 15 February and 31 March Sharplink's weETH rose by 17,018 ETH, against 16,740 deposited. Redemptions of 29,152 ETH paid on 3 to 5 July 2026 match Sharplink's LsETH falling to 181,299 ETH by 28 June; they went to a different address, which passed them to 0x3356…6836, the account that first funded the group.

Limits. No document names these addresses. Apart from the Ethereum Foundation's transfer, the identification rests on the timing and on the match with Sharplink's figures. LsETH redemptions reach their owners through a forwarding contract shared by other holders, so the link to the July 2026 redemptions is weaker than to the earlier ones. The account that funded the group had been used once before, in 2022. The 21 addresses hold about half of the 589k native ETH Sharplink reported in March 2026; the rest has not been found. The decision would change with a Sharplink disclosure these balances do not fit, or with evidence that a wallet in the group holds ETH for someone else.
