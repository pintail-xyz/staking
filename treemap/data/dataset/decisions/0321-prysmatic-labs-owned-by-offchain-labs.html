Offchain Labs owns Prysmatic Labs from 12 October 2022.

Operator or ownerOwned by Offchain Labs from 2022-10-12, for Prysmatic Labs · decided 2026-10-29

Prysmatic Labs is owned by Offchain Labs from 12 October 2022.

Grounds

  • Third partyOffchain Labs' acquisition of Prysmatic Labs was reported on 12 October 2022
  • On chainLido's registry renamed operator 1/27 "Prysm Team at Offchain Labs" on 10 November 2023

Explanation

Offchain Labs, the developer of Arbitrum, acquired Prysmatic Labs, the team behind the Prysm consensus client, in October 2022. Lido's registry renamed the team's operator "Prysm Team at Offchain Labs" on 10 November 2023. Prysmatic Labs is shown as owned by Offchain Labs from the report of the acquisition; who runs and allocates its operators' stake is unchanged.

What would change it

A later sale of the Prysm team, or a statement that the acquisition did not complete.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
