Bitwise owns Chorus One from 24 February 2026.

Operator or ownerOwned by Bitwise from 2026-02-24, for Chorus One · decided 2026-10-28

Chorus One is owned by Bitwise from 24 February 2026.

Grounds

  • First partyBitwise announced its acquisition of Chorus One on 24 February 2026
  • First partyChorus One joins Bitwise Onchain Solutions, Bitwise's staking business

Explanation

Bitwise Asset Management announced on 24 February 2026 that it had acquired Chorus One, a staking provider, which joins Bitwise Onchain Solutions, Bitwise's staking business. Chorus One's operators keep the names Lido's registry gives them; from that date they are shown as owned by Bitwise. Who allocates their stake is unchanged.

What would change it

A later sale of Chorus One, or a Bitwise statement that the acquisition did not complete.
