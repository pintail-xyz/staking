Decisions behind the attributions

Each attribution in the dataset rests on recorded decisions: which contracts and accounts identify a staking service, and which withdrawal addresses or validators belong to whom. Every decision states what was decided, the grounds and evidence for it, its sources, and what would change it, so that anyone holding other evidence can test it. Stake is as of 2026-10-30; peak stake is the most that rested on the decision at any weekly frame since 2020.
