Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x8e60…8224 are attributed to Binance's staking as allocator. In October 2026 about 76,900 active validators, some 3.54M ETH, withdraw there.

Binance's proof-of-reserves page lists the wallets behind its reserve report of 1 October 2026. One of its Ethereum hot and cold wallets, 0x32e1…1149 (80,000 ETH at the report), itself made 40 beacon deposits between 21 and 28 April 2026, every one naming 0x8e60…8224 as the withdrawal address. A second account, 0x2d59…b177, made 146 deposits naming the same address between 1 April and 22 October 2026, 290,032 ETH in all; every ETH it received came from five wallets on the same list, among them 0x28c6…1d60, and it has sent ETH nowhere but the deposit contract.

This confirms decision 0006, which identified the address through Binance's deposit account 0xbdd7…32a8 and the wBETH token: all of that account's execution-layer deposits name 0x8e60…8224, and the address has sent its withdrawn stake only to the staking address set in wBETH's contract. The address itself is not on Binance's list, which covers wallets holding liquid assets; a validator's stake sits on the beacon chain, not in its withdrawal address. Whether Binance runs these validators itself is not established, so no operator is recorded.

What would change it

A later Binance reserve list or statement placing 0x8e60…8224 with a client, or evidence that its validators belong to someone other than Binance.
