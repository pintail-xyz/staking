Explanation

The contract 0xbf15…c178 is the batch deposit contract behind the in-wallet ETH staking of the imToken wallet, whose validators InfStones runs. Validators first deposited through it are attributed to InfStones as allocator and operator; the stakers keep their own withdrawal addresses, and imToken, as the wallet through which they stake, never holds the stake. 4,493 deposits, about 144k ETH, went through it between April 2021 and October 2026, from 550 sending accounts; about 1,700 validators were active in October 2026 with some 55k ETH.

What InfStones and imToken say. InfStones announced in May 2021, and imToken likewise, that InfStones establishes a validator for every 32 ETH staked through imToken's wallet. imToken's guide says users buy 32 ETH nodes from their own address and pay a service fee to InfStones. imToken's progress update of 31 May 2021 reported 164 validators with 5,216 ETH staked; this contract had by then taken exactly 5,216 ETH.

On chain. The contract is stakefish's open batch deposit code with a fee per node, and its owner, 0xc8a6…53b8, created it and withdraws the fees. That owner funded the owner account of 20 private SSV operators, under which almost all of this contract's validators were registered. It also traded ETH with, and delegated to, the owner of InfStones' validator on Polygon. 176 of the 550 senders have traded through Tokenlon, imToken's in-wallet exchange.

Why InfStones is the allocator. Stakers choose InfStones' service through the wallet, and InfStones' key owns the contract and takes the fees; this is the rule applied to providers' depositor contracts (decision 0005), as for Kiln's Ledger Live contract (decision 0035). Neither company names the contract or its owner, and the contract can be called by anyone, though its fee makes that unlikely. The decision would change with an InfStones or imToken statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys InfStones does not run are deposited through it.

What would change it

An InfStones or imToken statement naming another contract, or evidence that keys InfStones does not run are deposited through it.
