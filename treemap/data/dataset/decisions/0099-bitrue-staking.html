Grounds

Explanation

Validators deposited by and withdrawing to 0x4fd7…717d are the staking of Bitrue, the exchange, with Bitrue as allocator. The address sent the deposits of 302 validators between June 2023 and August 2026, all with itself as withdrawal address, and no one else has deposited to it; 116 were active in October 2026. The stake is Bitrue's customers'.

What Bitrue published. Bitrue's help centre names its insurance fund's Ethereum wallet (June 2020, and again in its statement on transparency of November 2022), and announced that all its ERC-20 deposit addresses would change during maintenance on 5 August 2020, between 04:00 and 12:00 UTC.

The hot wallets. The insurance wallet was funded in June 2020 by 0x3487…d13e, an exchange hot wallet. On 5 August 2020, inside the announced maintenance window, a new wallet, 0x6cc8…fd21, received its first ETH from 0x3487…d13e; within two hours it was receiving customer funds, while 0x3487…d13e's traffic fell sharply and stopped by January 2021. The two wallets share over 700 counterparties: the same customers. 0x6cc8…fd21 supplied all the ETH that 0x4fd7…717d deposited, and 0x4fd7…717d sends its withdrawals to two wallets that pay only 0x6cc8…fd21.

So the stake is funded by, and returns to, the hot wallet Bitrue switched to on 5 August 2020. No operator is recorded.

Limits. The hot wallet is tied to Bitrue by its funding of Bitrue's published insurance wallet and by the timing of the switch, not by name. A community-maintained tracker listing these wallets as Bitrue's is recorded as a lead. The decision would change with a Bitrue statement contradicting it, or evidence that the address or its funding wallet holds ETH for a client.

What would change it

A Bitrue statement contradicting it, or evidence that the address or its funding wallet holds ETH for a client.
