Explanation

The contract 0xf7b6…238b is PufferProtocol, the core contract of Puffer's liquid staking token pufETH. Validators whose first deposit it sent, and validators withdrawing to the EigenLayer pods those deposits name, are attributed to Puffer pufETH as allocator. It deposited 11,532 validators between May 2024 and October 2025, holding as much as 290k ETH in October 2024; about 300 were active in October 2026.

Identification. Puffer's deployment scripts give this address as PufferProtocol on Ethereum mainnet. Puffer's documentation describes PufferProtocol as the contract with which node operators register validators, after posting a pufETH bond and buying validator tickets, and which funds each validator with 32 ETH from the pufETH vault. On chain, the validators withdraw to EigenLayer pods whose owners are Puffer modules, and each module names this contract as its PufferProtocol.

Why Puffer is the allocator. Puffer's node operators join without permission by posting a bond; Puffer is the service to which stakers handed their ETH, and the protocol assigns it to operators by its own rules. Puffer is therefore shown as the allocator, as for Stader's permissionless pool. The operators are not recorded. Before this decision most of these validators were unattributed; a few had been attributed to operators by earlier decisions or by graffiti.

The decision would change with a Puffer statement naming another contract, or evidence that validators deposited through it are not funded from the pufETH vault.

What would change it

A Puffer statement naming another contract, or evidence that validators deposited through it are not funded from the pufETH vault.

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
