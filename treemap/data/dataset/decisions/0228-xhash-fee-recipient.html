The fee recipient of validators deposited through XHash's own batch contract and of validators signing XHash.com; otherwise unattributed validators paying it are taken to be run by XHash.

This address is XHash's fee recipient; otherwise unattributed validators paying their fees to it are run and allocated by XHash.

Explanation

The address 0xaecd…0983 is XHash's fee recipient: validators XHash runs pay their transaction fees and MEV to it. Validators otherwise unattributed whose blocks paid it are attributed to XHash as allocator for as long as they did. They held as much as 45k ETH, mostly in 2023 and 2024; 89 were active in October 2026.

Who pays it. 3,216 validators have paid it since September 2022. 845 of them were deposited through XHash's own batch deposit contract, which XHash's documentation names (decision 0206). 848 belong to HTX's staking, for which XHash is among the operators, and 36 to Puffer; those keep their own allocators, since a fee recipient claim yields to every other allocator claim. The remaining 1,432 were unattributed. Validators of several unrelated stakers pay it, so it is the operator's address, not a client's. The address itself also deposited 45 validators in 2022.

The graffiti. Of the 13,301 blocks paying it, 7,715 were signed "XHash.com" and 5,751 carry SSV's default graffiti; only seven carry anything else.

Why these validators needed this decision. XHash's "Quick Mode" deposits go directly to the beacon deposit contract, so its batch contract does not cover them. Their "XHash.com" graffiti was not enough on its own, because the same validators, or their withdrawal addresses, also propose blocks with SSV's default graffiti, and a name in one block of a distributed validator identifies only the member that proposed it.

No document names the address, and its outgoing payments go mostly to two accounts not identified. The decision would change with evidence that validators XHash does not run also pay this address, or that it belongs to one of XHash's clients.

What would change it

Evidence that validators XHash does not run also pay this address, or that it belongs to one of XHash's clients.

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
