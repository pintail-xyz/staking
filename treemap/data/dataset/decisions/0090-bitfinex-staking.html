Explanation

Validators withdrawing to 0x7713…35ec are Bitfinex's staking, with the exchange as allocator. 2,087 validators have withdrawn to it since April 2023, deposited by many accounts; about 1,630 were active in October 2026 with some 52k ETH. The stake is Bitfinex's customers'.

Identification. Bitfinex's published list of its wallets, released with its proof of reserves in November 2022, names this address as its ETH and ERC-20 hot wallet, and as its hot wallet on several other networks. A hot wallet is the exchange's own, so stake withdrawing to it is the exchange's staking. The list dates from late 2022, and nothing on chain suggests the address has changed hands since.

The validators' graffiti names their node operators (for example SSV clusters and Luganodes), not the allocator; they remain operators only. Explorer labels and a community-compiled reserve list are recorded as leads. The decision would change if Bitfinex's own disclosures stopped listing the address, or with evidence that Bitfinex holds the address for a client.

What would change it

Bitfinex's own disclosures not listing the address, or evidence it holds the address for a client.
