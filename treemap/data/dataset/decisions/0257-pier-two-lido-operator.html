Lido operator 1/36 is Pier Two's, since renamed MAVAN.

Operator or ownerOperated by Pier Two from 2024-08-24, for MAVAN · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's node operator 1/36 is run by Pier Two, from its renaming to Pier Two on 24 August 2024.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's registry added operator 1/36 as "Numic" in October 2023 and renamed it "Pier Two" on 24 August 2024
  • On chainIt was renamed "MAVAN" on 26 June 2026, after MAVAN bought Pier Two

Explanation

Lido's node operator registry, read on chain, added operator 36 of the curated module as "Numic" on 6 October 2023, renamed it "Pier Two" on 24 August 2024 and "MAVAN" on 26 June 2026, three months after MAVAN Holdings bought Pier Two (decision 0261). The operator is recorded as run by Pier Two from its renaming to Pier Two; before then, which company ran it is not recorded.

What would change it

Evidence that operator 1/36 was handed to a company other than Pier Two or its owners.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.

  • 0x14bd64e4…fdc3d88c — Operator 1/36 renamed "Pier Two"
  • 0x64e170b5…465b6b1c — Operator 1/36 renamed "MAVAN"
