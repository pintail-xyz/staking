Lido's node operator 1/1 is Jump Crypto's from Jump's acquisition of Certus One in August 2021.

Operator or ownerOperated by Jump Crypto from 2021-08-03, for Jump Crypto · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's node operator 1/1 is run by Jump Crypto from 3 August 2021, when Jump Trading acquired Certus One.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's registry added operator 1/1 as "Certus One" on 19 December 2020 and renamed it "Jump Crypto" on 30 June 2023
  • Third partyJump Trading acquired Certus One on 3 August 2021
  • On chainIt belongs to no operator group in Lido's metadata registry

Explanation

Lido's node operator registry, read on chain, added operator 1/1 as "Certus One" on 19 December 2020 and renamed it "Jump Crypto" on 30 June 2023. Jump Trading acquired Certus One on 3 August 2021, and its team joined Jump's digital assets group, later Jump Crypto. The operator is recorded as run by Certus One until that date (decision 0312) and by Jump Crypto from it. It belongs to none of the operator groups Lido's metadata registry declares.

What would change it

A Lido or Jump Crypto statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
