MetaMask's validator staking contract, though deployed by Kiln, is administered by Consensys, whose Safes are its only operator's fee recipient and its treasury; validators deposited through it are allocated by Consensys, not Kiln.

Explanation

The contract 0xdc71…bebd runs MetaMask's validator staking. Although Kiln's deployment account created it, Consensys administers it, and validators first deposited through it are attributed to Consensys as allocator and operator, while each MetaMask user keeps their own withdrawal address. 8,016 validators were deposited through it between January 2024 and September 2026; about 1,090 were active in October 2026 with some 35k ETH.

What the contract records. It is Kiln's staking contract code, but reading it on chain shows who holds its roles. Its administrator, which adds operators and sets fees, is a Safe multisig. Only one operator has ever been registered, itself a Safe, whose fee recipient is another Safe. The contract takes a 10% commission, of which 90% goes to the operator's fee recipient and the rest to a treasury account. Nothing on chain gives Kiln any role or fee since the contract was created.

Why Consensys. The administrator Safe is the withdrawal address registered by Consensys's node in Rocket Pool's Oracle DAO, a node whose member ID ("consensyscodefi") and URL Consensys set itself, admitted by the other members' vote, as read from Rocket Pool's registry (decision 0071). The treasury and the operator's fee recipient were both first funded by an account that also sent ETH to that Consensys Safe, and the Safe's creator first funded three of the fee recipient's owners. So the commission goes to Consensys. Coverage of MetaMask's launch in January 2024 (The Block, ForkLog) says MetaMask partnered with Consensys Staking to run the validators, and does not mention Kiln. MetaMask's bug bounty describes the contract as Kiln's on-chain staking code.

Who runs the nodes. The validators' client builds follow the fleet that runs Coinbase's own retail validators, release by release. Coinbase Cloud joined Kiln's on-chain platform in 2023 as an operator for self-custodial wallets, so Consensys may run these validators on Coinbase's infrastructure. No source names Coinbase for MetaMask, so Consensys remains the operator of record.

The decision would change with a Consensys or Kiln statement that Kiln chooses the operator, or an operator added that Consensys does not control.

What would change it

A Consensys or Kiln statement that Kiln chooses the operator, or an operator added that Consensys does not control.
