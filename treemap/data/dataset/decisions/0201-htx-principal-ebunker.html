HTX's withdrawal wallet 0xc15c handed 380 of its validators to Ebunker, whose own accounts registered them on SSV; HTX is their principal.

AttributionPrincipal HTX · decided 2026-10-25

HTX is the principal of the validators withdrawing to 0xc15c…8b8b; Ebunker is the allocator of those it registered on SSV (0205).

Grounds

  • First partyHTX's deposit account 0xd57d (0200) made all of 0xc15c's deposits, and they withdraw to it
  • On chainEbunker's own accounts registered 380 of them on SSV with Ebunker's operators (0205)

Explanation

HTX (formerly Huobi) is the principal of the validators withdrawing to the multisig wallet 0xc15c…8b8b: the ETH was HTX's customers', and HTX chose how it was staked. All 470 of these validators were deposited on 21 August 2023 by HTX's deposit account (decision 0200), and all have since exited.

Who allocated them. Ebunker's own accounts registered 380 of the validators on the SSV network, on clusters of operators Ebunker had registered under its own name (decision 0205), so HTX handed those validators to Ebunker, which is their allocator. The other 90 remain HTX's as allocator.

The wallet. 0xc15c…8b8b is a multisig wallet created through the same wallet factory, by the same key, as HTX's other withdrawal wallets, and that key was first funded by reserve wallets that HTX's proof of reserves signs for (December 2022, May 2023).

The decision would change with evidence that Ebunker ran these validators for someone other than HTX, or that the wallet is not HTX's.

What would change it

Evidence that Ebunker ran these validators for someone other than HTX, or that 0xc15c isn't HTX's.
