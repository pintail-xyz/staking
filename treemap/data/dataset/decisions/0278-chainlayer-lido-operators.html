Lido's operator group 4.13 ("Chainlayer") and the operators it lists are run by ChainLayer.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.13, "Chainlayer", from 2 August 2026
  • On chainIt lists Lido operators 1/15, 4/17
  • On chainThe first of them, 1/15, was registered on 14 January 2022

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.13, "Chainlayer", from 2 August 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/15 ("ChainLayer"), 4/17 ("Chainlayer - PTO"). The group's name is matched to ChainLayer, and its operators are recorded as run by ChainLayer from the first one's registration on 14 January 2022.

What would change it

A Lido or ChainLayer statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
