Six withdrawal addresses whose rewards are swept together to 0x2bc1…8d56 belong to one staker.

Merge · decided 2026-10-29

The validators withdrawing to these six addresses belong to one staker, as its rewards all go to one collecting account, 0x2bc1…8d56.

Grounds

  • On chainAll six addresses send their ETH to 0x2bc1…8d56, which passes everything on to one account
  • On chainThey do so in the same four rounds (22 December 2024, 26 March 2025, 27 August 2025, 12 May 2026), each within about 150 blocks, and in the same order in the first three

Explanation

Six withdrawal addresses, holding 456 validators (15,000 ETH) in four clusters, send their withdrawn rewards to the same account, 0x2bc1…8d56, which passes all of it on to a single account. The sweeps come in four rounds (22 December 2024, 26 March 2025, 27 August 2025 and 12 May 2026), each finished within about 150 blocks, with the addresses sweeping in the same order in the first three. Such coordinated sweeps are run by whoever controls all six addresses, so the four clusters are joined as one staker, at the reward level. The staker is not identified.

What would change it

Evidence that 0x2bc1…8d56 collects for several unrelated stakers, such as a service sweeping for its clients.
