Explanation

Dappnode is the allocator of the validators withdrawing to the Safe multisig 0x5201…b756, the staked endowment of TheDAO Security Fund. There are 34 compounding validators, holding about 69k ETH in October 2026. The fund itself is recorded as principal (decision 0175).

What the fund and the press say. The fund's site says roughly 69,000 ETH of its endowment is staked on decentralised infrastructure run by Dappnode, and that its seven curators sign a 4-of-7 multisig. Decrypt reported at the fund's launch, on 29 January 2026, that about 69,420 ETH would be staked.

On chain. The Safe's owners are another Safe with seven owners and a threshold of four, whose verified ENS name is thedaofund.eth, and a second Safe sharing an owner with it. The Safe made all its deposits itself on 1 and 2 February 2026: 69,420 ETH to the 34 validators. Of the validators' blocks, 2,060 of 2,389 are signed "validating_from_dappnode_TDSF", the rest blank.

The fund hired Dappnode to run the stake, so Dappnode is the allocator and operator; the fund is the principal. The decision would change with the fund naming another operator, or blocks of these validators signed by another one.

What would change it

The fund naming another operator, or blocks of these validators signed by another one.
