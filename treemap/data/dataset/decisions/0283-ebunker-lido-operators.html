Lido's operator group 4.2 ("Ebunker") and the operators it lists are run by ebunker.

Operator or ownerOperated by ebunker from 2023-10-06, for Ebunker, Ebunker, Ebunker - PTO, Ebunker - IODC · decided 2026-10-28

Lido's operator group 4.2 ("Ebunker") and the operators it lists are run by ebunker.

Grounds

  • On chainLido's metadata registry declares group 4.2, "Ebunker", from 31 July 2026
  • On chainIt lists Lido operators 1/34, 4/10, 4/11
  • On chainThe first of them, 1/34, was registered on 6 October 2023

Explanation

Lido's operator metadata registry, read on chain, declares operator group 4.2, "Ebunker", from 31 July 2026: Lido's own record of which of its node operators belong to one organisation. It lists operators 1/34 ("Ebunker"), 4/10 ("Ebunker - PTO"), 4/11 ("Ebunker - IODC"). The group's name is matched to ebunker, and its operators are recorded as run by ebunker from the first one's registration on 6 October 2023.

What would change it

A Lido or ebunker statement that these operators belong to another company.

Evidence

Transactions and contract calls anyone can repeat on their own node.
