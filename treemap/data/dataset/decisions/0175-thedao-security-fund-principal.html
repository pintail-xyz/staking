Explanation

TheDAO Security Fund is the principal of the validators withdrawing to the Safe multisig 0x5201…b756: the 69,420 ETH staked there is the fund's endowment, and the fund chose Dappnode to run it (decision 0174, which gives the evidence).

The fund's site says roughly 69,000 ETH of its endowment is staked with Dappnode. On chain, the Safe is owned by the fund's multisig, thedaofund.eth (a 4-of-7 Safe, as the fund describes its curators' multisig), together with a second Safe; it made the deposits itself and receives the withdrawals.

The decision would change with evidence that the Safe or thedaofund.eth is not the fund's.

What would change it
